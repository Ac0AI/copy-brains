## How Damon acquired PDF.ai and what it became

- Damon was a software engineer at Cisco who began side hustling during Covid to build his own business. He also founded Testimonial, a platform for collecting and managing testimonials.
- He acquired PDF.ai (originally looseleaf.ai) from its founder, Josh, in May 2023, and grew it beyond $50K MRR.
- The original MVP was a weekend project: chat with one PDF at a time, no monetization. Its announcement on social media created hype, which he treats as a form of validation.
- The listing for sale caught his attention, and he bought it.
- Although he is a programmer by day job, the web app stack was new to him. He self-taught frontend and backend skills from Udemy.

## Launch playbook: rebrand plus a $99 lifetime deal

- His "launch" was a rebrand to PDF.ai combined with adding monetization.
- To test whether anyone would pay, he offered a lifetime deal at $99.
- The first customer came within 24 hours of launching the LTD.
- The campaign ran 3 months and drew over 300 adopters.
- He says this gave enough validation for monetization plus strong financial support to invest in growth.
- He used the LTD revenue to hire a full-time engineer to work on the product.

## Domain name as the main marketing channel

- Damon's top marketing effort is the relevant domain name.
- The domain tells Google explicitly what the product does, and the product is exactly what the domain says.
- Most traffic is organic, from people searching "PDF AI", and PDF.ai ranks at the top.
- His takeaway: a good, relevant domain gives a head start on competing for difficult keywords.

## Tool stack for running the business

- OpenAI for the AI features
- Vercel for web hosting
- Deel to manage global pay for remote employees and contractors
- Mercury for banking
- Stripe for payments

## What Damon would do differently

- He describes his growth so far as the very traditional route: keep building features, share updates publicly on social media, and wait for virality "like hitting a jackpot".
- He would explore more marketing channels.
- He would collaborate with influencers in their respective spaces, citing Jenni AI's TikTok marketing with various influencers as "quite ingenious" for rapidly increasing brand awareness.
- He would set up more partnerships with companies in related industries.
- He would do outreach to help older, legacy industries adopt AI technologies.
