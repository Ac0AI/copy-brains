## Cost savings from cloud and digital tools
- Ross says founders resist spending on digital tools, especially early on, but that focus on avoiding expenses makes them overlook what the tools offer. There is "a digital tool for just about everything," from customer interaction to internal coordination to market research.
- His argument: adopting current technology early creates "more chances for integration, development and prosperity" later.
- Cloud storage and cloud computing let a business run without office space, which means zero utility expenses. He frames this as especially relevant as remote work grows.
- Closing logic: these tools save time and may remove the need for extra hires, freeing time and budget to expand.

## Communicating with customers and staff
- Ross recommends workplace-communication platforms to keep staff informed, and internal company email for frequent, important updates to the whole team.
- He says these tools help hold staff accountable and keep everyone moving the same way.
- For customers, he calls email a strong channel for regular news, special offers, or general company updates sent to the client list.

## Choosing task and project-management software
- Ross says task-management tools range from standard time-management systems to paperwork and analytics functions. What they share is helping you "get more done in less time."
- Most are cloud-based, so people in different locations can collaborate from a computer or mobile device.
- Features differ, and most are paid, so he advises deciding which tasks you need help with before buying. Example: for a remote team, pick project software with strong collaboration features.

## Building brand awareness with digital tools
- Ross defines brand awareness as market recognition of your business and products, ranging from customers remembering your name to clearly understanding what sets you apart from competitors.
- His reasoning: a strong, reputable brand is critical, because you cannot grow clients and revenue if the target audience does not know you.
- Visual marketing is central to brand building in the social media era. He points to Canva, which offers templates for social-media images, status updates and other visuals.
- He also suggests social-media-management tools to post to many profiles at once and optimize digital content.

## Expanding the market with analytics, SEO and CRM
- Ross says growing a market takes more than email and social: use inventive technology that gives an edge in lead generation.
- Google Analytics is a "must-have" for tracking site traffic, internal links and the regions visitors come from.
- Paid all-in-one SEO programs can analyze organic-search rankings for your keywords and show what rivals have done.
- CRM software, built to manage customer relationships, is an option he recommends for expanding the customer base.

## How to pick tools
- Ross notes busy owners juggle many responsibilities, and tools, services and platforms exist to help run the business.
- His process: first decide what you want to accomplish, then spend time researching the best solutions for that goal.
