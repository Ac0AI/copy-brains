## Starting from zero: a four-step LinkedIn plan to build a niche brand

Justin divides his career into three phases: years of failures and setbacks, a turnaround after finding passion at work, and then applying those lessons to his first business. In May 2024 he wrote that if he were starting over today, he would run a simple four-step plan to focus on what he loves, get attention, build a following, and position himself to land business. He built it for LinkedIn because he thinks it is "the easiest place to grow a brand and business right now," but says it could be executed on another social platform.

## Step 1: Find the niche at the intersection of skill, love and willingness to pay

- Start by identifying the intersection of three things: something you're good at, something you love to do, and something people are willing to pay you for.
- His own starting niche was teaching founders how to build sales teams to sell their software to physicians. He notes this is very different from what he teaches now, but it is how he started as an entrepreneur.
- Sharpen the niche by adding a unique angle. His example: someone passionate about public speaking who is also an introvert could position as "Confident Public Speaking for Introverted Professionals."
- The test is whether people with that specific profile would pay to become as good a speaker as you are.

## Step 2: The "Teach, Teach, Teach, Sell" content framework

- In the beginning, you need to make people aware you exist. He calls LinkedIn the dominant platform for getting attention right now.
- The framework balances content: about 80% of the time you provide value to the audience, and about 20% of the time you softly promote your expertise, products or services.
- It runs as a 5-post cycle: three teaching posts (he later lists four in his example), then a soft sell. His public speaking example:
  - Post 1 (Teach): a step-by-step guide to structuring a compelling presentation.
  - Post 2 (Teach): the top 5 breathing exercises to calm nerves before a presentation.
  - Post 3 (Teach): a framework introverts can use to remember their speech on stage.
  - Post 4 (Teach): a guide to handling audience Q&A at the end of a speech.
  - Post 5 (Sell): a soft pitch for coaching services, stressing the unique approach for introverted professionals, ideally weaving in your story.
- Followed consistently, this builds the "trust, expertise, and authority" he says you need in a niche to generate leads for a coaching business.

## Step 3: Engage daily with the "$0.18 Strategy"

- The original is Gary Vaynerchuk's "$1.80 Strategy": leave your "two cents" on 9 relevant posts across 10 hashtags every day ($0.02 x 9 posts x 10 hashtags = $1.80).
- Justin calls that overkill. He used a simpler version when starting on LinkedIn, the "$0.18 strategy": leave your two cents on 9 relevant posts per day. He says to adapt it as you wish.
- Benefits: more visibility, new and relevant connections, and making your name, face and brand "a regularly occurring character on the platform."
- How to do it:
  1. Find 9 posts (or 9 people) in your LinkedIn feed relevant to your niche.
  2. Leave a thoughtful comment on each. Avoid "good post" or "agree." Share insights and experiences, tell stories, add knowledge that expands the post, or ask a good question to drive discussion.
  3. Repeat daily for a year to build relationships and visibility.
- The more your comment is the most relevant one, the more people see your name and face, "and that's how a following gets built."

## Step 4: Track results and adjust

- "None of this stuff matters if you're not tracking what's working and what isn't."
- Use LinkedIn Analytics or Shield to track post performance and overall presence.
- Look for which content types, subjects, styles, structures, days or times generate the most engagement, and adjust.
- Pay attention to the demographics of people interacting with the content and tailor content to their interests and needs. If you don't know what people want, ask them.
- Keep refining based on data and follower feedback.

## Principles behind the plan

- No strategy is perfect and there are a million ways to start building a brand, but following these four steps while "stay[ing] committed to helping your target audience" should create traction on LinkedIn and a serious presence in a niche.
- Order matters: pick the niche first, then teach consistently, engage with the audience daily, and let analytics steer.
