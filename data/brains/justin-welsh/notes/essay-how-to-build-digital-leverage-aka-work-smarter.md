## Digital leverage: the three-part formula

Justin defines "Digital Leverage" as using the internet to achieve more with less time, effort and resources. He treats it as essential for a one-person business, where there are only 24 hours in a day.

- He frames it as the difference between mastering your work and your work mastering you.
- He rejects "Hustle 24/7" culture: "The biggest flaw of the 24/7 hustle culture is the misconception that if you work hard enough, you will succeed in the end. You win with leverage. Not brute force."
- The formula has three parts:
  - **Content:** Think 1x, Publish 10x
  - **Selling:** Publish 10x, Sell 100x
  - **Compounding:** Sell 100x, Compound 1,000x
- Weekly action step: find one spot in your online presence or business where digital leverage could help, and commit to trying it for 30 days.

## Think 1x, Publish 10x: one idea becomes many pieces

Justin's best content starts with a single idea, then gets multiplied.

- **Finding the idea:** with his audience in mind, he researches trending topics, reads industry insights, and engages on social media for an hour every day.
- **Creating the core piece:** after choosing a topic, he writes one robust long-form piece. It might be a newsletter issue, an article or a Twitter thread. The goal is for it to be packed with value for the target audience.
- **Breaking it up:** he splits the long piece into smaller, more digestible pieces such as tweets, LinkedIn posts, Twitter threads and Instagram posts. One big idea becomes about 10 small pieces.
- He points to his Content OS as the system he uses for this repurposing.

## Publish 10x, Sell 100x: distribution as passive selling

Once he has about 10 small pieces from one idea, he publishes them in every place that makes sense for each piece (Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram and so on).

- **Why it sells:** more high-value touch points across platforms means more chances to engage the audience, build trust, and position yourself as an expert and authority.
- **Trust drives purchases:** the more your audience values your free content, the more likely they are to invest in your paid offerings.
- **Repurposing reaches more people:** turning one strong piece into bite-sized posts for different platforms reaches a wider audience with minimal effort.
- **The "trojan horse" tactic:** use the free social posts to promote products and services. Simple ways include:
  - linking back to your website
  - promoting a testimonial
  - encouraging people to read an article or blog post that links to your products or services
- He calls this "passive selling" and says it works much better, in his opinion, than aggressively promoting yourself all the time.

## Sell 100x, Compound 1,000x: affiliate programs

Justin treats 100 sales as more than a milestone. He sees it as the chance to compound sales through incentives.

- **The idea:** incentivize happy customers to spread the word. With 100 customers, you have a small army who can send you more customers.
- **The math:** if each of 100 original customers brings in 10 new customers, that is 1,000 additional sales.
- **Why referrals work:** potential customers trust a recommendation from someone they know more than any landing page or website.
- **The mechanism:** an affiliate program where customers earn a commission on every new customer they refer.
- **His commission rate:** he pays 35% rather than the standard 20%, because he wants people to "feel empowered to earn."

## Three requirements for a win-win-win affiliate program

Justin says an affiliate program needs three things:

1. **A high-quality product or service.** People only refer friends if they truly got value from the offering.
2. **A good enough incentive.** You can't pay out pennies and expect people to keep the program top of mind.
3. **Transparency.** Explain how the program works, when people get paid, and give helpful tips for promoting or talking about the products.

His summary: get content, selling and compounding right, and you see more happy customers, more revenue and better efficiency, using internet tools strategically to keep the business moving without constant brute force.
