## Cris's reason for backing Dragoș Pătraru's Biblioteca Nației

- Cris spent a Saturday in Ploiești supporting Dragoș Pătraru's new project, Biblioteca Nației. He treats Saturday as the start of the week, reserved for his most meaningful, mission-driven work.
- He had watched Pătraru's TV show with his dad as a child. Sitting at the same table as an adult, he thought: "As a child, I watched him from a distance. Today, I sit beside him, part of the same purpose."
- Pătraru's directness is what Cris admires most. He says things as they are, with no polite silence, and Cris says that takes courage.

## Investing in what's missing instead of debating what's broken

- Pătraru reinvests his energy, resources and potential into projects that help people. One result is Teatrul Nației.
- Teatrul Nației is a hub with Biblioteca Nației, a restaurant and a coffee shop. All are staffed by people who have faced hardship at home, at work or in life, and Cris says Pătraru gave them second chances rather than just jobs.
- The hub has rooms for rest and healing: a space to sleep, a ping-pong table, areas for therapeutic and recreational activities, and massage. Cris says healing comes from feeling supported in a place that cares, not only from words.
- Cris contrasts this with how most people spend money. He says many invest in "expensive things that we don't actually need, collecting objects while the world collects problems."
- Pătraru instead invests in people: their education, their growth, and a legacy "rooted in community, knowledge, and care" that will outlast us.
- Cris is inspired that Pătraru, his wife and his team "took nothing and built everything that matters," changing thousands of lives through hard, lasting work.

## Integrity means making tough choices daily

- Cris calls Pătraru "the conscience of Romanian media" at a time when many others have lost theirs. He sees him as more than a journalist or TV host.
- Watching him over the years taught Cris to live with integrity. In Cris's words, that is not just saying the right things but making tough choices every day.
- Integrity means facing the issues most people avoid and choosing what's right even when it's unpopular.
- Through the show Starea Nației, Pătraru gave people a reason to pay attention again. Cris argues that journalism at its best "is not entertainment. It's public service."

## Making people feel responsible rather than passive

- Cris says Pătraru's power is that he makes you feel responsible. You can't just consume content and complain, and he won't let you settle for "well, that's just how things are."
- Cris's call to action is: "Don't be passive. Don't be indifferent. Be awake. Be loud. Be human."
- His question to the reader: if one man with a mic and a mission can hold power accountable, what could all of us do together if we chose courage over mediocrity?

## The Thunder Way: ordinary people who choose to act

- Pătraru will speak at The Thunder Way, Thunder's first major event. Cris says he couldn't imagine launching it without him.
- Cris's launch principle: when you build something that matters, you need voices that carry weight, ones people trust, respect and find genuine. He treats landing Pătraru as something that seemed impossible until he found the courage to ask.
- The event's theme is ordinary people. Cris argues that "none of us are born special or destined for greatness" and that what sets some people apart "is not talent or luck, but choice."
- The people the event celebrates saw something wrong and couldn't walk away. They saw injustice and didn't wait for someone else to fix it. They took the leap while everyone else was still thinking.
- The moment he wants to showcase is when ordinary people stop being bystanders and become "the change they want to see."
- He closes with: one honest, unafraid voice can shape the narrative for a whole society, and it can "remind the rest of us that silence is not an option."
