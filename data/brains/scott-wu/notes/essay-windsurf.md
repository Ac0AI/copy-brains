## Deal terms and what Scott acquired

- Cognition signed a definitive agreement to acquire Windsurf, the agentic IDE (announced 2025.07.14).
- The acquisition covers:
  - The Windsurf IDE, "now with _full_ access to the latest Claude models".
  - Windsurf's IP, product, trademark and the brand it built.
  - $82M of ARR, with enterprise ARR doubling quarter-over-quarter.
  - 350+ enterprise customers and hundreds of thousands of daily active users.
  - World-class GTM, engineering and product teams, which Scott calls "above all" the most valuable part of the deal.
- Scott's stated rationale: combining Devin's rapid adoption as the leading fully autonomous agent with Windsurf's IDE product and "scaled GTM machine" will be "a massive unlock". At the time, Devin was already a top contributor for many enterprise engineering teams.

## Treating acquired employees well

- Scott says one of his top priorities in structuring the deal was to "honor their talent, hard work, and accomplishments". He and Windsurf's Jeff worked together on the terms.
- Every Windsurf employee gets the same package:
  - 100% participate financially in the deal.
  - 100% have vesting cliffs waived for work to date.
  - 100% receive fully accelerated vesting for work to date.
- New employees are to be treated like existing ones: "with transparency, fairness, and deep respect for their abilities and value."
- His framing for a merged team: "There's only one boat and we're all in it together."

## Announcing an acquisition: sequencing and communication

- Scott discussed the deal at an all-hands first, then sent the team a note the morning the agreement was signed.
- The information was highly confidential until 11am PT, after Windsurf's all-hands.
- He asked the team to stay quiet until then, "out of respect for our new colleagues". The Windsurf team "deserves to hear this from Jeff and me in the right way."
- He recapped the deal terms plainly (what is owned, ARR, customers, employee treatment) before the vision.
- The note closes by crediting his own team's work ("it's your hard work that has brought us here"), acknowledging "the work won't be easy", and asking everyone to give new colleagues "a warm welcome".

## Integration plan and operating approach

- Near term: the Windsurf team keeps operating as it has been. Cognition stays focused on accelerating customers' engineering with Devin.
- Over the coming months: Cognition will "invest heavily" in integrating Windsurf's capabilities and unique IP into Cognition's products.
- After the announcement, the aim is to work "as a united and aligned team".

## Mission and positive-sum framing

- Cognition's mission: "building the future of software engineering." Scott says the deal lets the company "move even faster" on it.
- His view of where engineering is heading: "Within our lifetime, engineers will go from bricklayers to architects, focusing on the creativity of designing systems rather than the manual labor of putting them together."
- He calls the deal "a huge win for all of us in the positive-sum game we're playing", and says "there's never been a more exciting time to build."
