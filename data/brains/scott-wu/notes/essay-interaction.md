## Poke: a personal agent that lives in your texts

- Scott describes Poke, made by The Interaction Company of California, as "a personal agent that lives in your texts."
- It messages the user first, follows up, and "feels less like software and more like a friend."
- Scale he cites: more than 100 million messages exchanged in the last three months. It is the only AI agent approved to text natively on Apple Messages.
- Reach: "hundreds of thousands of people" use it as "a partner in everyday life."
- The traits he credits for the product's appeal: it is proactive, it knows you, and it is fun to talk to.

## Why Scott applies the Poke model to Devin

- Scott says the Poke traits (proactive, knows you, fun to talk to) are "exactly how working with Devin should feel."
- He frames the acquisition as combining two audiences. Devin is used by some of the world's top companies for software engineering. Poke is used by consumers in everyday life.
- The goal he states is to shape how humans and AI collaborate, and to build that experience together with the Interaction team.

## The shared bet: always-on cloud agents

- Scott says the two teams have made the same bet from the beginning: always-on cloud agents.
- Cognition's role after the deal is infrastructure and models. Scott says "Cognition's models and infrastructure will make Poke even faster and more reliable."
- Poke users continue using the product as before, with no disruption at announcement.

## How Scott evaluates a team to acquire

- Prior relationship: Scott knew and admired Marvin (the Interaction founder) and his team for years. Scott and Walden were early angel investors in Interaction.
- Traits he values: "exceptional people who move extremely fast and aim for only the most ambitious products."
- Product conviction comes first. He opens by saying that if you have used Poke, you know why they are excited, so a product people love is the core signal.
- Alignment on strategy matters: the teams were already placing the same long-term bet before the deal.

## Deal facts

- Announced 2026-07-23: The Interaction Company of California joins Cognition.
- No price or terms are disclosed.
