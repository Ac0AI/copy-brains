## Structure of Alex's Money Models training

The page captured is the course navigation for Acquisition.com's "Money Models & Offer Stacks" lesson. The lesson text itself was not captured, only the module outline and a lead form. The outline still shows how Alex organizes the material: a money model is built from a sequence of offers, grouped into four types.

The four types of offers, each with its own module group:
- Attraction offers
- Upsell offers
- Downsell offers
- Continuity offers

The "Money Models" group opens with "Money Models & Offer Stacks", then "4 Types of Offers", then "Ride Along Apprenticeship".

## Foundations covered before money models

The course teaches the unit economics first, in this order:
- Context
- How Businesses Make Money
- CAC (customer acquisition cost)
- Gross Profit
- Payback Period
- CFA

The sequence suggests Alex wants a business owner to understand acquisition cost, gross profit and payback period before designing offers. The page gives no definitions or numbers for these terms.

## Offer types and the named tactics under each

**Attraction offers** (bring in new customers):
- Win Your Money Back
- Free Giveaways
- Decoy Offers
- Buy X Get Y
- Pay Less Now
- Free with Consumption

**Upsell offers** (sell more to a customer who is already buying):
- Classic Upsell
- Menu Upsell
- Anchor Upsell
- Rollover Upsell

**Downsell offers** (keep the sale when the first offer is declined):
- Payment Plans
- Free Trials
- Feature Downsells

**Continuity offers** (recurring revenue):
- Continuity Bonus Offers
- Continuity Discounts
- Waived Fee
