## The AI affiliate shift: Greg's thesis on affiliate marketing in the AI era

Greg argues that AI is about to redefine affiliate marketing, a space he describes as "quietly driving tens of billions of dollars every year." The core mechanic is simple: you earn a commission for each purchase made through your referral.

- He frames this as a paradigm shift, and says "when paradigm shifts like this occur, generational wealth is created."
- His prediction (written Nov 2023): "Thousands of AI affiliates are about to be born in 2024. A new job for a new era."
- He says he wishes someone had told him this earlier in his career, and now takes these moments seriously.

## Greg's history of affiliate marketing in four eras

Greg treats each era as a change in how affiliates get traffic.

- **1995-2002, early affiliate marketing:** Websites carried affiliate links. During the dotcom boom people figured out referrals would be a big part of the web. Most traffic came from portal websites and search.
- **2002, ad arbitrage:** Ad networks like Overture, Yahoo and Google Ads meant you no longer needed to own a website. People bought keywords and redirected traffic to landing pages.
- **2009-2022, social arbitrage:** Facebook ads, with targeting based on interests and likes, became "ad arbitrage 2.0." Influencers turned into media companies and "walking affiliate billboards," blending native advertising with their content.
- **2024 onward, AI arbitrage:** The new era he says is transforming the landscape.

## The arbitrage principle: rent attention instead of owning it

Greg's explanation of ad arbitrage is a reusable model for how affiliates make money.

- "You didn't need to own the attention, you could just rent it."
- The math: if it costs $10 to buy a customer but you get paid $15, "you'd do that all day long."
- His example: buy Google Ads keywords for "dating nyc" and send the traffic to an eHarmony landing page that pays $5 CPL (cost-per-lead).
- He sees AI as the next form of this arbitrage, where the cost of producing the attention-getting content drops sharply.

## Four ways AI is changing affiliate marketing

Greg lists four channels for the AI affiliate.

- **Faceless social:** AI lets personal and brand accounts regularly produce written posts and animated or video content on YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok. AI tools write scripts, animate and compile videos, so marketers can make high-quality, engaging content at scale for affiliate marketing.
- **Personalized AI chatbots:** "Chatbots are the new search." He expects OpenAI's GPTs to be embedded throughout the internet. If billions of people find content through bots, those bots become "the new wild west in affiliate marketing," acting as personalized sales assistants that recommend products based on user interactions.
- **AI responses:** Crafting insightful or entertaining AI-driven responses can boost internet presence, attract followers and direct attention to affiliated products.
- **AI niche websites:** AI-written SEO articles can dominate search rankings, which he calls "a game-changer for organic search marketing."

## AI-assisted SEO workflow, with BoringMarketing as the example

Greg calls BoringMarketing.com "the king of this whole AI-assisted SEO movement," and says they use AI to bring in millions of unique visitors from organic search. He discloses that he is a customer and an investor in the business.

Their process, as he describes it:
- Use AI research agents to create content briefs.
- Use LLM automations to generate first drafts.
- Apply AI-assisted human editing.

His summary: it is "using a team of robots to make the whole SEO process more streamlined."

Result he cites: he saw them take a SaaS app from 8k to 80k organic visitors in a month, which he calls 10x growth in a month.

## What separates winners from the pack

Greg's closing view is that technical access to AI will not be the differentiator.

- "The real winners will be those who blend its capabilities with a deep understanding of human behavior, not just algorithms."
- "It's the creative and empathetic use of AI, not just its technical prowess, that will set the true innovators apart."
- He ends with: "The rise of the AI affiliate is just beginning..."
