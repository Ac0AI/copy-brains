## The four eras of work and the apprenticeship model

Greg lays out a staged timeline for how AI changes work. He says the future of work is about to get "wild", millions of jobs will change, and a golden era for entrepreneurship is starting.

- **Human-powered (now):** Humans decide and do the work. Software like Figma or Google Docs is just a tool.
- **AI-assisted, human-powered (2025-2026):** AI is a sidekick ("Iron Man and Jarvis") that handles grunt work such as data analysis, scheduling and routine tasks. Humans focus on strategy, creativity and decisions. "We become the captain, and AI becomes our GPS."
- **AI-agent powered, human-assisted (2027-2030):** Agents run complex operations, make real-time decisions and optimize processes. Humans oversee, guide and refine. Greg says everyone will have "100s of agents working for them".
- **Humanoids, AI-agent powered (2030 and beyond):** Software plus hardware. Robots cook, clean, build and care for the elderly. Humans design, supervise and improve the machines, and spin up businesses around them. Everyone becomes a "humanoid entrepreneur".

He summarizes the progression with David Fuesser's apprenticeship model for AI:
1. I do, you watch.
2. I do, you help.
3. You do, I help.
4. You do, I watch.

## Examples of the AI-assisted and agent eras

- A doctor uses AI to analyze patient data faster and more accurately. The AI suggests diagnoses, and the doctor makes the final call.
- Short-form video creation becomes about 100x faster with AI, so the creator focuses on the creative part while AI handles the rest.
- A financial advisor's AI agent manages client portfolios: it makes investment decisions, tracks market trends and adjusts strategy on the fly. The advisor steps in for big-picture planning and personal client interactions.
- Greg says the AI-assisted era "will kill the middleman".

## Don't skip steps: the McDonald's and IBM lesson

Greg's first takeaway is "Don't do what IBM and McDonald's did."
- McDonald's hired IBM and spent millions on an AI drive-thru in 100 restaurants.
- The tech wasn't good enough. It couldn't understand accents.
- The mistake was jumping from human-powered straight to AI-agent powered. "Skipping steps is risky."

## Buying businesses and AI-ready niche data

**Acquiring businesses with AI:**
- Greg says acquiring businesses "just got REALLY interesting".
- The play: buy a business at 3X EBITDA, add AI to make it 10X EBITDA within 12 months. He calls it "an infinite money glitch".
- Then either flip it and retire, or keep it cash-flowing and use that to buy the next one.

**Niche data as the moat:**
- "The best AI businesses don't have AI yet but have the best niche data."
- The real opportunity is in traditional businesses with proprietary data that haven't adopted AI.
- Three ways in: license the data and incubate something, acquire the company, or do a deal to bring them into the AI age.

## New models and industries Greg expects

- **AI agents hiring humans for edge cases:** Agents manage tasks but hire humans for complex, nuanced situations, like "AI managers with human assistants". Greg acknowledges this sounds weird but believes it's the direction.
- **New industries:** Humanoid robots create demand for robot maintenance, robot training and even robot psychology. The job market expands in directions "we haven't even imagined yet".
- **Automation as the new AI:** Entire workflows will be automated, like a thermostat set to 70°F that keeps the house right without attention. Greg says business will be mostly automated.

## What to do now

- **Experiment with AI tools yourself.** Greg tries 2-3 new tools per week: play with agents, build GPTs. There's big upside in getting good at them, and it "allows you to see the world in a new way".
- **Incentivize your team to play with AI tools.** They may resist because they see AI as competition or change, but the more they use it, the more upside for the business.
- Greg calls it "the wild west", full of opportunity. It fires him up.
- His closing thesis: "Anyone can become an entrepreneur." Soon you'll just need the right AI behind you and the right idea to drive toward.
