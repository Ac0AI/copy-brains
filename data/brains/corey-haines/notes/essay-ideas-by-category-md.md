## Content and SEO: ideas Corey lists for organic growth

Corey's marketing-ideas reference lists ten content and SEO plays.

- **Easy keyword ranking:** target low-competition terms competitors overlook, such as niche variations, long-tail queries and emerging topics.
- **SEO audit:** run a technical SEO audit of your own site and share the findings and fixes publicly to build authority.
- **Glossary marketing:** build a glossary of industry terms. Each term becomes a page targeting "what is X" searches.
- **Programmatic SEO:** build template-driven pages at scale around keyword patterns with search volume. Examples are location pages, comparison pages and integration pages.
- **Content repurposing:** turn one piece into many formats. A blog post becomes a Twitter thread, YouTube video, podcast episode or infographic.
- **Proprietary data content:** use unique data from your product to publish original research. Data competitors can't replicate creates linkable assets.
- **Internal linking:** link strategically to distribute authority and improve crawlability, and build topical clusters of related content.
- **Content refreshing:** update existing content with fresh data, examples and insights. He says refreshed content often outperforms new content.
- **Knowledge base SEO:** optimize help docs for search. Support articles aimed at problem-solution queries capture users actively seeking solutions.
- **Parasite SEO:** publish on high-authority platforms (Medium, LinkedIn, Substack), which rank faster than your own domain.

## Competitor and comparison plays

- **Competitor comparison pages:** write detailed "[Your Product] vs [Competitor]" pages. They capture high-intent searchers.
- **Marketing jiu-jitsu:** turn competitor weaknesses into your strengths. When competitors raise prices, launch affordability campaigns.
- **Competitive ad research:** study competitors' ads with tools like SpyFu or the Facebook Ad Library to learn what messaging resonates.
- **Importers as marketing:** build "Import from [Competitor]" tools to reduce switching friction.
- **Free migrations and contract buyouts:** offer free migration from competitors, or pay to exit their contracts.

## Free tools and engineering as marketing

Corey groups these as useful products that lead naturally to the paid product.

- **Engineering as marketing:** build free calculators, analyzers and generators that solve real problems.
- **Calculator marketing:** ROI calculators, pricing estimators and savings tools attract links and rank well.
- **Quiz marketing:** interactive quizzes engage users and qualify leads. Personality quizzes, assessments and diagnostics generate shares.
- **Scanners:** free tools that audit something, such as website scanners, security checkers and performance analyzers.
- **Chrome extensions:** an extension with standalone value makes the Chrome Web Store another distribution channel.
- **Side projects and microsites:** small related tools attract users who may later convert. Dedicated microsites for a campaign, product or audience can rank faster.
- **Public APIs:** open APIs let developers build on your platform and create an ecosystem.
- **Template marketing:** give away free templates users can use immediately.

## Paid advertising channels

Corey lists twelve paid channels and gives one angle for most of them.

- **Podcast advertising:** sponsor relevant podcasts. Host-read ads perform especially well.
- **Pre-targeting ads:** run awareness ads before direct-response campaigns, because warm audiences convert better.
- **Facebook/Meta ads:** use detailed targeting, test creative variations and use retargeting.
- **LinkedIn ads:** target by job title, company size and industry. He says premium CPMs are justified by B2B purchase intent.
- **Reddit ads:** reach passionate communities with authentic messaging. "Transparency wins on Reddit."
- **Google ads:** capture high-intent searches across brand, competitor and category terms.
- **Quora ads:** an intent-rich environment where users are asking questions your product answers.
- **YouTube ads:** video ads with detailed targeting, using pre-roll and discovery placements.
- **Instagram ads:** visual-first, for products with strong imagery. Stories and Reels capture attention.
- **Twitter ads:** reach engaged professionals through promoted tweets and follower campaigns.
- **Cross-platform retargeting and click-to-Messenger ads:** follow users across platforms with consistent messaging. Messenger ads open a conversation instead of a landing page.

## Social, community and email

- **Community marketing:** build communities around the product on Slack, Discord or Facebook groups.
- **Reddit:** mine it for the real language your audience uses and their pain points, and participate authentically. "Provide value first."
- **Quora:** answer questions with genuine expertise and mention the product only where natural.
- **Personal brands and short video:** build a LinkedIn personal brand for B2B reach, an X presence through threads and insights, and Instagram behind-the-scenes content. Use TikTok, Reels and Shorts for new audiences.
- **Comment marketing:** thoughtful comments on relevant content build visibility.
- **Engagement pods:** coordinate with peers to boost each other's content.
- **Mistake emails:** send "oops" emails only when something genuinely goes wrong. Authenticity generates engagement.
- **Founder welcome email:** a personal welcome from the founder creates connection.
- **Lifecycle emails:** use onboarding sequences to guide users to activation. Reactivation, win-back and trial-reactivation campaigns recover churned users and expired trials, which "aren't lost causes".
- **Newsletters and capture:** send a consistent monthly newsletter, use dynamic email capture (exit intent, scroll depth triggers) and fix deliverability through authentication and list hygiene.

## Partnerships, referrals and product-led growth

- **Affiliate discovery:** find potential affiliates by analyzing who links to competitors. Run a structured commission affiliate program.
- **Reseller and expert networks:** let agencies resell with white-label options, and build networks of certified experts who implement the product.
- **Newsletter swaps and sponsorship:** swap promotional mentions with complementary newsletters, or sponsor relevant communities, newsletters and publications.
- **Integration and partner tactics:** run joint marketing with integration partners, share remarketing audiences through pixel sharing, and create shared Slack channels with partners and customers.
- **Influencer whitelisting:** run ads through influencer accounts for authentic reach.
- **Referrals:** use two-sided referrals that reward both referrer and referred. Reward referrals with earlier access during launches, and build referrals into newsletters.
- **Product mechanics:** "Powered by [Your Product]" badges give free impressions. One-click OAuth registration minimizes signup friction. Viral loops encourage sharing, and in-app upsells prompt upgrades.
- **Onboarding and offboarding:** use concierge setup for high-value accounts and continuously improve onboarding. Optimize cancellation flows to retain customers or learn why they leave.

## Launches, events, PR and creative plays

- **Launch tactics:** use Product Hunt and alternatives (BetaList, Launching Next, AlternativeTo), early-access pricing, and seasonal promotions (Black Friday, New Year). Giveaways and lifetime deals generate attention, leads and cash.
- **Events:** run live webinars, virtual summits, roadshows and local meetups. Speak at, sponsor or host conferences.
- **PR:** pitch newsworthy stories, use fundraising announcements for coverage, contribute expert quotes to journalists (HARO), and acquire newsletters, podcasts or publications in your space.
- **Content formats:** online courses, a book, annual reports, personalized end-of-year wraps, podcasts, public changelogs, public demos and industry interviews.
- **Unconventional plays:** industry awards, viral challenges, grants, product competitions, controversy (positioning against industry norms), humor, marketing stunts and out-of-home ads. He calls Moneyball marketing "data-driven marketing finding undervalued channels".
- **Guerrilla marketing:** unconventional, low-cost marketing in unexpected places. He points to a direct-mail framework with three rules (relevance, relationship, precision), ROI discipline and a named case library.
- **Platforms and developer plays:** open source, app store optimization, marketplace listings (Salesforce AppExchange, Shopify App Store), review and directory sites (G2, Capterra, GetApp), YouTube reviews and a YouTube channel, developer relations, certifications and support as marketing.
- **Localization:** expand internationally with localization, and adjust pricing for local purchasing power.
- **Customer language:** use the exact words your customers use in your marketing.
