## Don't marry your idea: most startups fail, so stay flexible

- Marc launched 20+ startups, and only 1 made good money and grew.
- He says the same pattern holds for most solopreneurs who made life-changing money.
- His takeaway is to keep launching and not get attached to any single idea. "When it's time to focus on your startup, you'll know."
- Context: in 2023 he built 9 startups, and his income jumped from $1,500 in January to $65,000 in November.

## Ditch free plans: the math doesn't work for solopreneurs

- Marc cites that only 3% of users upgrade to paid plans.
- At that rate, you need 10,000 users to make $3,000 from a $10/month subscription. He calls that "an insane amount of work for a solopreneur."
- He argues "Grow first, monetize later" is a strategy for VC-backed startups, not bootstrapped solopreneurs.
- Implication: charge from day one instead of building a large free user base.

## Start with one-time payments, add subscriptions only if there's demand

- Marc killed a startup by swapping one-time payments for recurring ones.
- His reasoning: monthly passive income is good for business owners only, and users are much more reluctant to subscribe.
- His rule: "Start with one-time payments. Add subscriptions if there's demand."

## Sell painkillers, not vitamins

- Same traffic, very different results:
  - 100,000 visitors on a nice-to-have product website made $6,000.
  - 100,000 visitors on a must-have product website made $100,000.
- Unless you already have an audience, he advises avoiding note-taking apps and habit trackers.
- Principle: "People buy solutions to painful problems."

## Emotions sell: write copy around the outcome, not the feature

- Example: few people care about a NextJS boilerplate, but many care about a shortcut to ship startups faster and reach financial independence.
- Tactic: rephrase your copy so it triggers an emotional reaction, rather than describing the technical product.

## First impressions decide conversion

- The first impression is the website headline, the YouTube thumbnail, or the first sentence of a tweet.
- Marc claims that one sentence has more impact on conversion rate than anything else.
- Practical implication: spend disproportionate effort on the headline, thumbnail or opening line.

## Protect your health to avoid solopreneur burnout

- Marc says a solopreneur wears 10 hats (developer, copywriter, accountant and more), so burnout is always close. His formula: "Food + Sleep + Workout = Psychiatrist."
- **Eat healthy:** avoid carbs, quit sugar and alcohol.
- **Exercise daily,** even if it's an easy 15-minute workout.
- **Make sleep priority #1:**
  - Sleep at the same time every day, in a dark room at 18°.
  - No coffee in the 10 hours before bed.
  - No water in the 3 hours before bed.
  - Dim the lights 1 hour before bed.
