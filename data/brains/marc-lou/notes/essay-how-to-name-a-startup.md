## Marc's three ways to name a startup

Marc lists three common approaches:

1. A super easy, short name, like Stripe or Butter. He notes ChatGPT is good at generating these.
2. An SEO-oriented name, like HeadshotPro.
3. A benefit-oriented name, like NomadList or Tweet Hunter.

He focuses on the third. In his words it is "the easiest way to be memorable as a solopreneur with limited resources." These names are usually made of 2 short words.

## The two-word formula for a benefit-oriented name

- **Word 1: relatable to the customer.** It should "speak a language they use and understand."
  - Tweet Hunter: professionals on Twitter
  - ShipFast: indie makers
  - OurBabyAI: couples
- **Word 2: the change in the customer's life.** It "shouldn't be about your product at all." The questions to ask are why the product is useful and how it changes the customer's life.
  - ShipFast: finally launch that side project
  - CleanShotX: take beautiful screenshots with no skills
  - NomadList: go nomad, change your life

## Generating name candidates

1. Take the two words (the audience word and the life-change word).
2. Find 5 short synonyms for each (e.g. quick becomes fast, launch becomes ship).
3. Ask ChatGPT to create all combinations of the two lists of 5.
4. Remove combinations that are over 10 words (as written in the source). Marc's stated principle is "Keep it simple."

## Picking the winner: the memory test

1. Pick your 3 favorite candidates.
2. Send them to a few friends and ask "What's your favorite?" Marc says their answers don't matter yet.
3. After 24 hours, remove your messages from the chat.
4. Ask the same friends "Which one do you remember?"
5. The most remembered name is the new startup name.

The goal is an unforgettable name people will search on Google, because "first impression matters."

## Don't over-invest in naming

- Marc used to spend days brainstorming a startup name and "finally came up with something nobody understood."
- His advice now: "Don't spend too much time on this. A name won't make a startup. Pick one, and start building!"
