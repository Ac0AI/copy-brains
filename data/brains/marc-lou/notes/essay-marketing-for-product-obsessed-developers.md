## Free tool marketing as a distribution strategy

- Marc never enjoyed marketing but says he would not earn $50K/month without some kind of it. His alternative is making free tools that "fulfill my inner builder".
- He defines free tool marketing as "symbiosis": launching free, viral mini-apps to promote the main startup.
- Examples he cites: AI Tweet Generator (free) promoting Tweet Hunter (paid), and his own free logo maker LogoFast promoting ShipFast. LogoFast was nominated for the Product Hunt Golden Kitty Awards 2023.
- He launched 7 free tools in one year.

## Why free tools work

- They expand the audience to a wider market.
- They rank for new SEO keywords.
- They are shareable, so people spread the word on platforms like TikTok.
- Launching often grows an audience.
- They create a good first impression that warms up potential customers.
- They keep the builder motivated to work.
- Example: the User Persona tool went viral on TikTok and added 226% revenue for MakerBox.

## Guidelines for building a free tool

- The app must be free, because that is how the viral loop is created.
- One feature is enough, and it must be super simple.
- The name should say exactly what the app does, so people remember it and search for it on Google.
- The experience must start on the homepage. Visitors should not have to sign up or click links.
- The tool can live on the main website, but a dedicated domain is best.
- SEO setup example: LogoFa.st has its own domain and is also hosted at ShipFa.st/tools/logo-fast. The canonical URL points to the ShipFast page to boost the main site's domain authority.

## How to find free tool ideas

- Take a standalone feature of the main startup and release a free version. This works best when the startup already has traction, since the demand for that feature is known.
- Use free tools to validate potential features. Instead of cluttering the main product, launch the feature as a free tool. If the market validates it, the feature can still be added later.
- Example: Ahrefs' domain authority checker is a free tool that is also a feature in their paid plan.
- Alternatively, get into the customer's journey and think about what they need before they use the product. Example: after realizing that 15 minutes of reading a day means 19 books a year, people might want a habit tracker, so a free "tiny habits" tool can feed a habit tracker.

## Converting free tool traffic into paying users

- The simplest way is to advertise the startup inside the free tool. A simple ad placement can get up to 10% click-through rate.
- Numbers climb when the tool embeds seamlessly in the customer's journey, so the promotion feels like an extension of the tool.
- Example: a free tiny-habits tool automatically imports the user's habits when they sign up for the habit tracker. The conversion rate from free tool traffic to the habit tracker is 30%.
- Alternative: collect emails and market through a follow-up sequence.

## Launch, profit, repeat

- Free tools often get media coverage and go viral on TikTok thanks to their built-in virality.
- Marc says to "launch everywhere": Hacker News, Reddit, Twitter, Product Hunt. "The more noise you make, the higher the revenue."
- He uses viral launch videos (via his product LaunchViral), so each product goes viral on X, gets a Product Hunt badge, and is reshared on other social media.
- Once a tool is launched, move on to the next one. Each launch makes more noise, increases daily traffic, and grows the startup, while keeping the inner builder satisfied.
- Other channels he lists for micro startups: launching on Reddit, Hacker News, Product Hunt, and programmatic SEO.
