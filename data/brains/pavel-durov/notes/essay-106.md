## Self-restraint as a productivity and willpower system

- Pavel calls himself "a big believer in self-restraint."
- For the 15 years before this 2019 post he had no alcohol, caffeine, meat, pills or fast food.
- He credits this with health results: he had a fever only once in 15 years and "typically, I just don't get ill."

## Escalating diet restrictions to train discipline

- About a year before the post he added more limits: no gluten, no dairy, no eggs, no fructose.
- His stated aims were higher productivity and clarity of thought, plus training willpower and self-discipline.
- In May 2019 he cut his diet to fish and seafood only.
- He recommends that diet for boosting productivity if you have daily access to fresh wild-caught fish.

## The "seagan" evolutionary argument

- Farmed meat and agricultural products entered the human diet only about 15,000 years ago.
- Wild fish cooked on fire is something our ancestors "evolved to consume throughout the last million of years."
- Humans needed daily water, so they lived near rivers and lakes.
- He therefore finds a seagan (fish and seafood) diet makes "much more sense" than veganism or rawism from an evolutionary perspective.

## Ice-cold swimming as a procrastination cure

- Every winter in Finland or Switzerland he swims in ice-cold water to improve willpower.
- Having stayed for minutes in a lake with a thin layer of ice on top makes you "less likely to procrastinate when it comes to starting on a boring but necessary project."
- The logic is that doing something deliberately hard makes starting unpleasant work easier.

## Water fasting for clarity and product work

- In the month of the post he went further and consumed no food at all.
- He had been on a water fast for 6 days and felt great.
- He says zero food consumption improves clarity of thought, and he got many things done on the product-management side.
- He argues fasting lets the digestive system "clean and reboot" and frees the immune system from constantly clearing incoming food.
- Hunter-gatherer ancestors went long periods without food, so he believes the body expects a break in consumption at least once a year.
- He notes most religions have a fasting tradition because it is "healthy and necessary both for the body and the mind."

## Motivation: Telegram users come first

- He accepts he may lose some muscle mass from fasting.
- He considers the trade worthwhile if he comes up with great new ideas for Telegram during the fast, since they would benefit "all of the millions of Telegram users."
- He states that making users' lives more enjoyable "has been and will be my number one priority."
