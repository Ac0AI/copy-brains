## Telegram's monetization goal: financial sustainability without losing independence

- When Telegram launched, Pavel set the end goal as a financially sustainable project that "can serve humanity for decades (or centuries) to come."
- He frames monetization as a means to independence. Revenue pays for servers, traffic and wages, which keeps Telegram building features without outside control.
- His principle: "While some other apps consider their users a tool to maximize revenue, we consider revenue a tool to maximize value for our users."
- At the end of 2024 he restated it: the purpose of monetization is to ensure users "will always remain our #1 priority."
- He argues the 2024 results show that social platforms can be financially sustainable while staying independent and respecting users' rights.

## Privacy-conscious advertising

- Telegram started monetizing in late 2021 and reviewed the first year in December 2022.
- Ad rules he set:
  - No personal data is used for targeting.
  - Promoted messages are shown only in public one-to-many channels.
- Despite these limits, he says Telegram's ads "significantly outperformed the market" and put the company on a steady path to financial sustainability.
- Ad revenue increased "a few times" in 2024.
- In 2024 he announced that ads would be sold exclusively for TON. Anticipating the concern that Telegram would hold too much Toncoin, he set a cap:
  - Telegram's share of TON is to stay at about 10% of supply.
  - The surplus goes to long-term investors under a 1-4 year lockup and vesting plan, at a discount to market price.
  - His stated aim is to lock up free-floating TON, stabilizing the ecosystem and reducing volatility.

## Telegram Premium: subscription growth

- Premium launched around mid-2022. By December 2022, five months in, it had passed 1 million subscribers.
- He called it one of the most successful social media subscription plans ever launched. At that point it was still a fraction of revenue, but he said it "may even rival our ads" one day.
- Milestones:
  - September 2024: 10 million paid subscribers.
  - December 2024: over 12 million, with the subscriber count having tripled during 2024.

## Expanding the monetization stack (2024)

Pavel listed the 2024 monetization innovations as:
- Stars
- Gifts
- Giveaways
- Mini Apps
- The Affiliate Platform
- Telegram Business
- Telegram Gateway

- In September 2024 he replaced People Nearby with "Businesses Nearby":
  - People Nearby was used by under 0.1% of users and had problems with bots and scammers.
  - Businesses Nearby shows legitimate, verified businesses that can display product catalogs and accept payments.
- He removes features that attract abuse and are barely used, and swaps in monetizable business features.
- He argues that the roughly 0.001% of users involved in crime damage the platform's image and put the interests of nearly a billion users at risk. He committed to turning moderation "from an area of criticism into one of praise."

## Profitability milestone (2024)

- 2024 was the first profitable year in Telegram's 3-year history of monetization.
- Figures he gave:
  - Total revenue surpassed $1 billion.
  - Year-end cash reserves exceeded $500 million, not counting crypto assets.
  - Premium subscribers exceeded 12 million.
- By March 2025 he described Telegram as having over 1 billion monthly active users and "substantial profit."
- The Telegram bonds issued in 2021 were sold when Telegram had zero revenue and half today's user base.

## Funding growth with bonds instead of equity

- March 2021: Telegram raised over $1 billion by selling bonds (debt) to large, knowledgeable global investors. He said this lets Telegram keep growing "while sticking to its values and remaining independent."
- Over the four years to end-2024, Telegram issued about $2 billion in debt.
- March 2024: a $330M bond issue.
  - It was oversubscribed.
  - Adjusted for the Federal Reserve rate, the terms were the most favorable in company history.
  - He read rising demand as global institutions valuing Telegram's audience and monetization growth, and as reinforcing its position to challenge the "Goliaths" of the industry.
- Autumn 2024: Telegram repaid a meaningful share of its debt, taking advantage of favorable bond prices.
- March 2025: the bonds traded at all-time highs, adjusted for the federal funds rate.
- May 2025: Telegram issued $1.7B of 5-year bonds at 9% interest, maturing June 2030.
  - More than $950M of the earlier bonds (due March 2026) were exchanged into the new issue.
  - The offering was expanded beyond the initial $1.5B because of strong demand, and demand still far exceeded what Telegram could allocate.
  - He said the proceeds leave "more than enough resources" to repay the remaining 2026 bonds.
- He credits the bondholders' support, which helps Telegram stay independent "in any economic environment."

## Ownership and control

- Pavel states bondholders are not shareholders and have no say in Telegram's decisions. He is the sole shareholder.
- Debt rather than equity is how he funds growth without giving up control.
- In January 2026 he rebutted claims of dependence on Russian capital. The $1.7B bond offering included "exactly zero Russian investors," and the 2021 bonds had been largely repaid.
