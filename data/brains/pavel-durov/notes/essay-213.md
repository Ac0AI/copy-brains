## Why Pavel resisted Stories, then built them anyway

- Pavel says "more than half of all feature requests" Telegram receives are about Stories.
- He was initially against building them, since Stories are "already everywhere."
- He argues Telegram "wouldn't be Telegram" if it ignored user demand or failed to "innovate on existing formats."
- Rule he follows: let user demand override personal skepticism, but never copy a format as is. Rework it to fit the product's identity.
- Internal testing converted skeptics on his own team: "We can no longer imagine Telegram without it."

## Privacy as the core differentiator

- Pavel makes privacy the first pillar of Telegram's version of an existing feature.
- Each story gets its own audience, with granular control:
  - Everyone
  - Only contacts (with exceptions)
  - A few selected contacts
  - A Close Friends list
- Any contact's Stories can be hidden into a "Hidden" list in the Contacts section instead of the main screen.
- Takeaway: when entering a crowded category, he differentiates on control for the user rather than on novelty for its own sake.

## Compact UI that protects existing screen space

- Stories sit in an expandable section at the top of the chat list.
- Pavel's stated goal: make them "easily accessible without taking away valuable space."
- Design rule he applies: a new feature should not displace the core experience (the chat list).

## Flexible expiry and permanent profile content

- Ephemerality is optional. Creators choose expiry of 6, 12, 24 or 48 hours, or display stories permanently on their profile page.
- Each option carries individual privacy settings.
- Pavel expects saved stories to make profiles "more informative and colorful."
- Business effect he points to: users can learn more about people they meet in groups or channel comments, which deepens connections beyond chat.

## Building on existing Telegram strengths

- Dual Camera Support builds on "the success of Telegram's Video Messages." Front and rear cameras record simultaneously.
- Captions add context, links and people tagging, alongside "dozens of powerful photo and video-editing tools."
- Pattern: he extends formats Telegram already won with rather than bolting on a clone.

## Stories as a growth and virality lever for channels

- Pavel says channels "will benefit from more exposure and subscribers" once messages can be reposted from channels to stories.
- His claim: this makes "going viral on Telegram" a lot easier.
- Distribution logic: add a sharing path from content inside the app to a high-visibility surface, so creators gain subscribers.

## Launch approach

- Feature was in its "last testing phase," with release planned for "early July" 2023 (post dated 2023-06-26).
- He announces it in advance via his channel, teases "more surprises," and follows with a preview in the next post.
- Framing: it "will herald a new era on Telegram, where it will become even more fun and social."
