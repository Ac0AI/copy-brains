## Respect for users as the core growth strategy

- Pavel says Telegram's "secret" is simple: "respect your users." He offers it to Facebook for free, joking that Facebook has dozens of employees working full-time on why Telegram is popular and could save "tens of millions of dollars."
- He contrasts this with WhatsApp's January 2021 Terms change, which required users to feed their private data to Facebook's ad engine. He credits that change with accelerating a user flight from WhatsApp to Telegram that had already been under way for a few years.
- At about 500 million users and growing, Telegram has become a major problem for Facebook, in Pavel's framing. His claim is that WhatsApp cannot compete with Telegram on quality and privacy.

## Zero marketing spend

- Pavel states that Telegram "doesn't spend any money, let alone billions of dollars, on marketing."
- His comparison point: Facebook spent almost $10 billion on marketing in 2019.
- His rationale: "We believe that people are smart enough to choose what is best for them." He treats roughly half a billion users as proof that this belief is justified.
- He describes the growth model as users choosing the product on its merits (quality and privacy) rather than being persuaded by paid promotion.

## Competitors' covert marketing and how Telegram responds

- Pavel accuses WhatsApp of switching to covert marketing because it cannot compete on quality and privacy. His evidence: Wikipedia editors exposed multiple paid bots adding biased information to the WhatsApp article. In December 2020 that article carried the label "This article may have been created or edited in return for undisclosed payments."
- He says Telegram has also detected bots spreading inaccurate information about it on social media.
- His response is public rebuttal with verifiable facts, not counter-spending. He lists the three myths and answers each with specifics (next two sections).

## Myth 1: "Telegram's code is not open-source"

- All Telegram client apps have been open source since 2013.
- Telegram's encryption and API are fully documented and, he says, have been reviewed by security experts thousands of times.
- Telegram is the only messaging app in the world with verifiable (reproducible) builds for both iOS and Android.
- By contrast, he says WhatsApp intentionally obfuscates its code, making its encryption and privacy impossible to verify.
- His principle: back trust claims with things users and experts can check themselves.

## Myths 2 and 3: "Telegram is Russian" and "Telegram is not encrypted"

**"Telegram is Russian"**
- Telegram has no servers or offices in Russia.
- It was blocked in Russia from 2018 to 2020.
- It is still blocked in some authoritarian countries such as Iran, while WhatsApp and other "supposedly secure" apps have never had an issue there. He uses this as evidence of independence.

**"Telegram is not encrypted"**
- Every chat on Telegram has been encrypted since launch.
- Secret Chats are end-to-end encrypted.
- Cloud Chats offer real-time secure and distributed cloud storage.
- He contrasts this with WhatsApp: zero encryption for a few years, then an encryption protocol funded by the US Government. Even if that encryption is solid, he says it is invalidated by multiple backdoors and reliance on backups.
