## Telegram's growth: zero marketing, word of mouth, and consistency

- Pavel credits Telegram's growth to word of mouth alone: "In just a decade, Telegram gained over 800 million active users purely thanks to word of mouth" (August 2023, on Telegram's 10th birthday).
- His stated "secret" is consistency (Feb 2021): "For the last 7.5 years we've consistently defended the privacy of our users and regularly improved the quality and feature set of our apps."
- He frames this as a general rule: "Anyone who stays true to their values and applies focused effort over a long period of time is bound to succeed in their area." He says this holds for sport, blogging, art, coding, business or studying.
- Growth was steady compounding: since the 2013 launch, the user base grew over 40% each year, and Telegram became the most downloaded mobile app in the world in January 2021.
- The assumption behind the product (April 2024): "people are inherently smart. And smart people will always choose the freedom and quality of Telegram over the restrictions and mediocrity of legacy apps."

## User milestones, 2021-2025

- Early January 2021: over 500 million monthly active users. 25 million new users joined in 72 hours. Their origin: 38% Asia, 27% Europe, 21% Latin America, 8% MENA.
- For comparison, Pavel notes that in the prior year about 1.5M new users signed up per day.
- 5 October 2021: a record day, with over 70 million "refugees from other platforms" in one day. The daily growth rate exceeded the norm by an order of magnitude.
- To retain surge users, he asked existing users to "say hi to their newly arrived friends, help them unpack, and let them know what we have in stock", and to make sure newcomers "stick around."
- July 2023: over 2.5 million new sign-ups every day and 800 million monthly active users earlier that year.
- April 2024: 900 million monthly users, with half opening Telegram daily.
- March 2025: "significantly over 1 billion" monthly active users, the second most popular messaging app in the world excluding WeChat. Each user opens Telegram 21 times a day and spends 41 minutes daily on the app.

## Growth driven by privacy and distrust of rivals

- Pavel attributes the January 2021 surge to users refusing to trade privacy for free services: "People no longer want to exchange their privacy for free services. They no longer want to be held hostage by tech monopolies that seem to think they can get away with anything as long as their apps have a critical mass of users."
- Telegram positioned itself as "the largest refuge for those seeking a communication platform committed to privacy and security," and he calls the surge possibly "the largest digital migration in human history."
- Independence is part of the pitch: "Unlike other popular apps, Telegram doesn't have shareholders or advertisers to report to. We don't do deals with marketers, data miners or government agencies." He cites a 2018 post saying Telegram had not disclosed "a single byte" of private user data to third parties since August 2013.
- He treats Telegram as an idea more than an app: "the idea that everyone on this planet has a right to be free."
- For newcomers during migrations, the message is that Telegram is "the largest independent messaging platform. We won't fail you when others will."
- On the competition (March 2025), he calls WhatsApp "a cheap, watered-down imitation of Telegram" and says it has copied Telegram's innovations while "burning billions on lobbying and PR campaigns to slow us down. They failed."

## Channels as a distribution lever

- Pavel argues Telegram channels are "the only direct way for opinion leaders to reliably connect with their audiences."
- Reason: "Unlike other networks, Telegram doesn't use nontransparent algorithms to decide whether a subscriber will see content they subscribed to or not." Removing manipulative algorithms "restore[s] transparency and integrity to public 'one-to-many' communication."
- Heads of state and public organizations joined the platform, which he presents as a trust signal. In January 2021 he listed the presidents of Brazil, Turkey, Mexico, France, Ukraine, Uzbekistan and Taiwan, plus the prime ministers of Singapore, Ethiopia and Israel.
- He says leaders and organizations rely on Telegram to counter misinformation and raise awareness. Verified accounts show a blue check mark in chat lists and search results.

## Funding growth and monetization

- Scaling costs money: July 2023 growth meant "higher expenses on storage and traffic." He says Telegram is "super efficient when it comes to expenses."
- Monetization started in 2022. In July 2023 he said Telegram was not yet profitable, which he called impossible after only the second year of monetization, but was closer to profitability in absolute numbers than Twitter and Snap.
- In July 2023, Telegram issued about $270 million of bonds as a tap of existing bonds, with well-known funds participating.
- Pavel personally bought about a quarter, "tens of millions", on top of "the hundreds of millions I spent over the last 10 years to keep Telegram operational."
- He rejects personal consumption: some suggested he buy a house or a jet, but he prefers to stay focused on work, owning only Telegram, some Bitcoin and some Toncoin.
- Profitability milestones: cash flow positive in Q1 2024, on track for a profitable 2024, and $547M in profit in 2024 (as stated in March 2025). He says this "solidifies our position as an independent company that puts its users first."

## Next phase and growth under bans

- August 2023: the next step is to "go beyond messaging and spearhead innovation in social media in general." The gradual roll-out of stories to all users marked the start of that stage.
- His stated goal is to use Telegram's popularity "to change the lives of billions for better."
- April 2026 on bans: although Telegram is banned in Russia, he says 65M Russians still use it daily via VPNs, with 50M+ sending messages every day.
- Iran banned Telegram years ago with a similar result: the government hoped for mass adoption of its own surveillance messaging apps but got mass adoption of VPNs. He cites 50M members of the "Digital Resistance" in Iran.
- His response is to keep adapting by "making Telegram's traffic harder to detect and block."
