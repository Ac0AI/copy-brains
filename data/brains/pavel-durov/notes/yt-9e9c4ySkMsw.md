## Pavel's origin story and why Telegram exists

- Pavel was born in 1984 and was taken to Italy at age four or five, so he experienced both a free-market, free-speech system and a planned-economy, restricted-speech one as a child. He recalls dozens of TV channels and hundreds of ice cream kinds in Italy, versus a few channels of Communist Party content and two or three ice creams in the late Soviet Union.
- He started VK, which became the largest social network in Russia and the post-Soviet states, in 2006.
- In December 2011, with hundreds of thousands protesting in Moscow, the prosecutor's office ordered him to shut down opposition communities on VK. He refused, published a scan of the request, and added a photo of a dog in a hoodie sticking out its tongue as his "official response."
- Armed police then stormed his apartment. He wanted to reach his brother and team and realized no tool was safe, because everything was monitored. He says even WhatsApp sent messages in plain text and added encryption only after Telegram launched.
- He started building Telegram to solve that problem. After Edward Snowden's 2013 revelations he concluded surveillance was a global issue and launched Telegram in 2013.
- With no marketing effort, it spread first in places where privacy and freedoms were at risk.
- He lost VK, selling it below market price after raids and criminal investigations. When Russia demanded encryption keys in 2017-2018, Telegram refused.

## Neutrality and consistent moderation principles

- Pavel says Telegram will remain a neutral platform that protects everyone regardless of political views or nationality: conservatives and liberals, communists and libertarians, Russians and Ukrainians, Palestinians and Jews. What matters to him is "consistency and the respect to basic human rights."
- He argues people view free speech in a one-sided way. They like it when you protect allies and dislike it when you also protect rivals.
- Examples he gives of equal treatment:
  - During COVID, Telegram protected Black Lives Matter movements and anti-lockdown protesters alike.
  - He recently refused a French police demand to shut down a public channel run by far-left protesters, because it looked legitimate, even though he doesn't agree with communist ideas.
- In Belarus, Telegram refused to censor dissenters. It also polled Belarusian users on who they voted for, and the results differed sharply from the official numbers.
- After China's large DDoS attack on Telegram during the Hong Kong protests, he called it "a badge of honor."
- He says no other social platform has spent as much effort and money defending freedoms in Eastern Europe.

## Privacy and security features Telegram pioneered

- Pavel says many features now standard in messengers arrived first on Telegram, and he points to stats and infographics on his Telegram channel.
- He lists these:
  - The self-destruct timer, launched in 2013, years before Signal and WhatsApp.
  - An additional passcode lock on the app.
  - Remote deletion of entire chats for all participants, which he says is unique among mass-market messengers. It helps if someone is apprehended by an authoritarian regime.
  - End-to-end encryption for millions of people, rolled out early, with WhatsApp following much later.
- Telegram is open source, so anyone can verify the encryption is what it claims to be. He says WhatsApp isn't open source, and with Signal you can't prove the iOS app matches the GitHub code.

## Balancing encryption with law enforcement

- Pavel's position is that "messages are sacred." Telegram never discloses a word of private messages.
- If there is a serious concern about an imminent violent crime, and with serious justification and legal documents, Telegram will disclose the IP address or phone number of the suspected user.
- Telegram does this consistently and publishes quarterly transparency reports.
- He says Telegram complied with all binding legal requests it received, and that his French detention (4 days in Paris in 2024) accused him of complicity in users' crimes even though he is a French citizen with a known address.
- He claims Telegram's moderation is more efficient than Instagram, Facebook and Snapchat, based on research the team did in France.
- He alleges that the head of French intelligence asked him to shut down certain channels around the Romanian election. France first denied it, then confirmed meetings but said they were about something else. Telegram takes no side in elections, and Pavel argues for transparency about requests and equal standards for all political players.

## Advice for founders: trust your intuition and experiment

- Pavel tells young entrepreneurs building for human rights to "trust their own intuition and not be afraid to experiment."
- His encryption example:
  - WhatsApp, Signal, Instagram, Facebook and Google mostly rely on the same encryption algorithm, developed more than 10 years ago with Open Technology Fund money that comes from the US Department of Defense.
  - If something is wrong with it, billions of people depend on a single point of failure.
  - Anyone developing their own standard will face heavy criticism, because cryptography experts tell you to use what everyone else uses.
  - He notes that history has examples of respected experts who turned out to be undercover agents promoting standards that served government goals.
- This is the logic behind Telegram's custom approach to encryption.

## Product positioning and growth vision

- Pavel says Telegram is the number two global messaging app with more than one billion users. He thinks it has good chances of becoming number one, and could reach three or four billion users within the next 10 years if it keeps doing what it's doing.
- He credits user love to technological superiority, independence, transparency and privacy.
- He keeps innovating on privacy and free speech.
- Differentiator he stresses: users subscribe to channels and sources not manipulated by algorithmic feeds. Most other platforms use non-transparent algorithms that decide what users see, while "on Telegram people are in control."
- He teased an announcement about integrating modern AI technology into Telegram within days.

## Blockchain and TON strategy

- He says he was a relatively early Bitcoin buyer (2013).
- He calls blockchain "the technology of freedom" that empowers people.
- Telegram designed its own blockchain architecture, the Open Network (TON), to solve scalability. He says it can process hundreds of thousands to millions of transactions, which matters for a platform like Telegram.
- Product uses of the chain:
  - Users can tokenize their usernames and own them directly, so no one, including Telegram, can take a digital identity away.
  - Users can log into Telegram with a blockchain wallet, without relying on a phone number.
- He says he is a big fan of Bitcoin and Toncoin in particular.
