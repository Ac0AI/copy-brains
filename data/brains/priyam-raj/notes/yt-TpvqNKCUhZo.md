## How Priyam earned his first internet paycheck at 16

- On 9 June 2017, at age 16, Priyam received his first online paycheck: $104.58 (about 6,600 Indian rupees at the time).
- He found out while returning from his JEE tuition and updating his passbook. He calls it "such a perfect day."
- The income came from blogging, and he says it is still possible today and has become easier since.

## His blog setup: niche, platform and domain

- His niche was gaming, specifically Clash of Clans. He wrote articles on war strategies, when to attack, and the best Town Hall 9 maps.
- He ran the blog on Blogspot, Google's own free blogging platform.
- He asked his brother to buy a custom domain, which cost 125 rupees for the first year. He connected it to the blog, replacing the free something.blogspot.com address, which "just didn't look so good." He calls it his first ever online purchase.

## Traffic and monetization

- His strategy articles got picked up by search engines. For a query like "best Town Hall 9 map" he ranked about third or fourth on Google Search, and that search traffic drove the blog.
- He monetized with Google AdSense. Approval of his blog was "another such a perfect day."
- Traffic came from many countries (India, the US and others), which kept income stable.
- He averaged about $400 to $500 a month, with peaks of about $900. He spent it on building his gaming PC.

## Choosing a blogging platform

- He advises against Medium. You don't own the blog, and Medium doesn't pay out in many countries; he believes India is one, but he isn't sure if that has changed.
- He recommends Blogspot for starting out. It is completely free, flexible enough to run AdSense, and looks good with the right themes and configuration. People underestimate it.
- WordPress is the more advanced option. It needs monthly paid hosting, so he suggests it only if you want to get serious about blogging rather than just give it a shot. He calls it very flexible: "you can do pretty much anything on it."

## Picking a niche and finding peers

- Choose a niche you are actually enthusiastic about, so you never deal with boredom and can write about things you already care about.
- ChatGPT can now help with the writing.
- Join Twitter communities. He has done internet entrepreneurship for a long time and uses the same approach for projects like SaaS startups: search Twitter, find communities and see what people are doing.
- For this kind of blogging (niche sites), search terms like "niche sites" or "niche site earnings" to find others doing the same and learn from them.
