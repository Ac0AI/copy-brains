## Repositioning a stuck product: Outrank from $300 MRR to $300k MRR

- Outrank (co-founded with Eugene) was a $300 MRR AI blog generator about 15 months before April 2026. Eugene had grinded on it alone with nothing moving the needle.
- Tibo joined in January 2025 and saw an "autonomous SEO machine" rather than a content tool. His takeaway: "Positioning beats features every time."
- The product was framed as "AI blog writer" in a sea of AI blog writers. It was repositioned as "SEO automation platform that builds your web presence while you sleep."
- "Different frame → Different outcome." The product barely changed; the framing did.
- Result by April 2026: $300k MRR, 2,500+ websites, 50,000+ articles generated per month.

## Finding a moat when content is a commodity

- Tibo argues a moat is a defense against uncertainty and competition, which matters especially in the age of AI.
- "Content alone has no moat." GPT, Claude and Gemini all produce readable articles, so writing quality alone gives nobody a reason to choose Outrank.
- The moat he built is a backlink exchange: every Outrank user joins a backlink network. When Outrank generates an article, the AI links to relevant posts from other users' sites.
- Links are contextual, not random. Example: an email marketing article links to another user's email subject line guide, and a SaaS metrics article links to your churn article. These are real backlinks from real sites in adjacent niches.
- On the Google penalty worry: most agencies insert links with zero relevance and Google detects that. Outrank only adds contextually matched links that add value for the reader, which Tibo says Google rewards.
- Reported results: 25,000+ backlinks added, 750,000+ articles created. One user after 49 days: "DR jumped from 5 to 25. All I did was set up the blog."
- Lesson: "Network effects turn commodities into moats." A backlink network that gets more valuable with every user can't be copied overnight.

## Why the network advantage compounds

- Tibo says network effects don't grow in a straight line. With 2,500 websites in the pool, each user can be matched with 2,500 potential linking opportunities.
- At 10,000 websites he expects value not just to quadruple but to "explode."
- Reasons he gives:
  1. More topical coverage gives better contextual fits.
  2. More niche diversity gives higher relevance scores.
  3. More volume gives more linking opportunities per article.
  4. The whole network's domain authority rises together.
- "We're not in the AI writing business. We're in the 'make sure AI can find you' business."

## Owning the narrative: "AI didn't kill SEO"

- During a period when viral X threads daily claimed "ChatGPT killed SEO," Tibo took the opposite view and built the messaging around it.
- His reasoning: ChatGPT doesn't magically know about your tool. It searches Google and Bing to decide what to recommend, so to be recommended by AI you need to own the web.
- Practical implication: more articles, more backlinks and more presence in search results. Pages with better rankings appear more often in AI responses, and one well-optimized article can show up in thousands of AI conversations.
- He says OpenAI's Atlas launch in early 2026 validated this: web presence is how you get into LLMs.
- Lesson: "Position into the fear, not away from it." Everyone said AI is killing SEO; Outrank said AI makes SEO more important than ever. "The narrative that wins gets the customers."
- The narrative also gave customers a reason to accelerate rather than pause.

## Outrank growth timeline and which levers worked when

- January 2025: $300 MRR, Eugene's original product; Tibo joins.
- March 2025: $10k MRR after a Product Hunt launch and repositioning as SEO infrastructure.
- May 2025: $40k MRR once the affiliate program kicked in. One affiliate made $3k in a single month.
- Late 2025: $200k MRR.
- April 2026: $300k MRR.
- Three levers by stage:
  - Early: distribution. Tibo had the audience and insights, Eugene had the product, and they launched.
  - Middle: product results. Users seeing measurable growth, plus testimonials.
  - Late: narrative. The AI-resilience argument.
- Lesson: "What works at $10k MRR doesn't work at $200k MRR."

## Pricing and product focus for Outrank

- Pricing is deliberately simple: $99/month for 30 articles. "No credits. No tiers. No confusion."
- That works out to $3.30 per article, and Tibo says the math works in their favor given everything included in the product.
- Tibo says he generally doesn't do discounts because they devalue what's been built. He made an exception with a 20% off first month code ('TIBO') for his newsletter readers.
- Next focus areas:
  - Content quality for ranking and for AI citation: deeper brand voice matching, richer article structures, more content formats. A monthly update aimed at articles that "sound like an expert wrote them, not a content mill."
  - Compounding the backlink network.

## The meta-lesson for builders

- Tibo credits Outrank's success not to AI writing ("Anyone can build that") but to two things:
  1. Network effects through contextual, relevant backlink exchange.
  2. Narrative positioning that turned AI fear into acceleration.
- His summary: "Find the moat. Own the narrative. Move fast."
- He ends by asking builders what they are building that might just need different positioning, implying a stuck product often needs reframing before more features.
