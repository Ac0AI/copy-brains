## Why Tibo is all-in on SuperX and X growth

- Tibo calls SuperX "the most important project I'm working on right now." He describes it as "what Tweet Hunter should be today if its new owners hadn't given up on it."
- He sold Tweet Hunter and Taplio in 2022 and left completely by 2024. He says the acquirer bought mainly for Taplio.
- He is "more of an X guy than a LinkedIn guy" and missed a tool that worked properly on X.
- His example of the acquirer's slow pace: when X launched long-form posts, Tweet Hunter took multiple months to ship support, while competitors had it in days.

## How Tibo came to own and rebuild SuperX

- SuperX was first built by Hieu. Tibo joined him and they built a Twitter extension that was "quite successful."
- Tibo felt it was hard to grow with only an extension and that the product needed a web app.
- Hieu had different goals and lost motivation. Because the project mattered so much to him, Tibo bought Hieu's shares and looked for a new partner.
- He found Rob in February. The start was slow because some foundations were missing, so the product needed a complete revamp.
- They worked in stealth mode for 6 months. Tibo says the product only then became "actually useful," and he now uses it daily to schedule content, find inspiration and write better posts.

## What growth on X requires: content plus a support network

- Tibo's framing of what you need to grow on X is two things: good content, and friends (a support network).
- He says SuperX is "not another schedule-your-content software." It aims to improve the content engine itself.
- The product is built around both sides: a Content Studio for content and a Social Hub for friends.

## The Content Studio: removing the blank-cursor problem

- It combines strong inspiration features with a dedicated content studio that helps users write better.
- The goal is that nobody stares at a blank cursor wondering what to tweet.

## The Social Hub: a first circle of 10 supporters

- Tibo says the Social Hub helps users build a strong first circle of friends who want to grow together, and that "that's how X actually works."
- His rule: you need a foundation of 10 incredibly supportive people who like and engage with everything you do.
- These people also support you during key moments of your X journey.

## Why Tibo is bullish on X as a platform

- He is "incredibly bullish on X right now," citing Nikita Bier joining as a product guy. He believes X will dominate the social media space.
- His stated bet: "Many creators will get rich on X. I want to double down there."

## Building products you need yourself, and launch approach

- Tibo says the product works and the foundations are solid, and he is seeing results from daily use. He frames this as "what happens when you build something you desperately need yourself."
- Before the public launch, he gathers feedback by asking readers about their biggest struggle growing on X.
- He builds an early-access list: readers reply "SUPERX" to be added.
- He runs a weekly newsletter (tmaker.io, 65k+ readers) and uses it as a distribution channel for his products, sign-off "Keep building."
