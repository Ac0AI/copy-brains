## Portfolio results in 2025: from $125k to $700k+ MRR

Tibo went from 5 projects at about $125k combined MRR to $700k+ MRR in 12 months (5.6x). He treats the portfolio model as the main lesson of 2025: revenue became "genuinely distributed" for the first time.

- While one product stagnated (Feather), four others grew. Revid was no longer about 95% of revenue.
- He says that financial security "changes how you think, build, and take risks."
- Product MRR, start to end of 2025:

| Product | Start | End |
|---|---|---|
| Revid.ai | $100k | $450k+ |
| Outrank.so | $12k | $250k |
| Feather.so | $10.8k | $10.3k |
| SuperX.so | $1k | $14k |
| PostSyncer.com | $0 | $2.6k |

- His summary lesson: "Build products that matter. Everything else follows."

## Build a "tailwind business" that gets better with every AI model release (Revid.ai)

Revid grew the most in absolute dollars, from $100k to $450k+ MRR. Each new model (Veo 3, Sora 2, Gemini 3.0) first felt like an existential threat. The opposite happened: "Every new model made Revid better, not obsolete."

Why it worked, per Tibo:
- Revid is not just a wrapper around AI APIs. It built workflows users need without making them play with AI on their own.
- Short-form video needs speed and quality, and AI provides both.
- The team focused on high-quality output and niche content where bigger players don't compete.
- Deep integrations with TikTok, Instagram and YouTube create switching costs.

He calls Revid a "Tailwind business": it gets sharper when a better AI model runs in the background. He argues not every business becomes irrelevant with AI growth. His question for founders: "How can I build a business that grows alongside the fast growing tech?"

## Make customers part of the growth loop: Outrank's backlink exchange network

Outrank grew from $12k to $250k MRR. Tibo says the team built something so valuable they didn't see it at first: the fastest, most effective way to get backlinks and grow Domain Authority. He says the harder problem is building authority that makes AI recommend you.

How the backlink exchange network works:
- Every user joins a pool automatically.
- Blogs reference relevant content from other users.
- Links add genuine value (not spam).
- Everyone's DR grows together, and network effects compound.

Tibo says this group growth model means customers win more and stay longer. He expects AI SEO to go mainstream, so more people will look for tools to grow their search presence. His question: "How can I make my customers an active part of my growth journey? Can this help them too?"

## Rescuing a stuck product with the right co-founder (SuperX)

SuperX went from $1k to $14k MRR. It sat at $1k MRR for 10 months with his first co-founder, Hieu, and he almost killed it.

What changed after Rob joined:
- They rebuilt from scratch with a new architecture and a better user experience.
- Rob's product storytelling "made it click."
- The product was repositioned for how X actually works in 2025.

Tibo says "the right co-founder changes everything" and that Rob brought the missing piece he couldn't provide alone. He says SuperX is better than Tweet Hunter ever was, and Tweet Hunter hit $250k MRR at peak.

The challenge ahead is X's expensive API, where every post fetch costs more than an AI generation. His diagnostic question: "Are the skills I lack hurting my business? Is there anyone who can help me here?"

## Handing off a stagnating product (Feather)

Feather was the only product that didn't grow ($10.8k to $10.3k MRR). Tibo says stagnation hurts more than failure because you keep hoping it'll turn around. He likes the product and says the tech is solid, but he couldn't crack growth.

- He made a deal with his business partner Pranav, who gets more shares and will run it fully on his own.
- He is looking for a GTM-focused person to join and scale it.
- His lesson: "You can't win in everything you do," and giving yourself that margin of error is okay. Sometimes the best move is handing a great product to someone who can focus on it.
- His prompt: "Am I working on a product that is not giving me the results I need? Is this time for a hard step?"

## Spotting ecosystem shifts (PostSyncer)

PostSyncer went from $0 to $2.6k MRR, with a recent explosion. Tibo's reasons it's working:
- Platform fragmentation is real; Threads overtook X in users.
- Creators need presence everywhere but can't manage it manually.
- AI makes deep multi-platform customization possible.

He believes it already does more than giants like Buffer and Hootsuite for multi-platform AI posting. His question: "What recent changes in the business ecosystem can I take advantage of?"

## Audience as the foundation, and what he optimizes for

Tibo says his business is largely built on his audience. Exposure to 170,000 followers helps validate ideas, find beta testers and get initial traction.

- **Social:** He stopped caring about follower count in 2025; only impressions and engagement matter. He focused on valuable content instead of growth hacks: "that's the real growth hack." He would rather have 1000 real builders than 10,000 random scrollers.
- **Newsletter:** His weekly email has 65,000+ subscribers, and 30,000+ read each week. He uses it to share behind-the-scenes of the business and invites replies.
