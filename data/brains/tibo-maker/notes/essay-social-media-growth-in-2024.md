## Why Tibo says social growth works differently in 2024

- Tibo's core claim: "every platform is becoming TikTok. And your followers don't matter."
- An account with 150 followers can go viral, and an account with 100k followers can stay "pretty much dead". He says he has many examples.
- He contrasts this with the old days, when a post like "Best websites you don't know about" could pull 30k+ likes.
- His view in August 2024 is that growth on Twitter and LinkedIn is slow, with low impressions and less engagement.
- Because followers matter less, content quality decides reach. In his words, "No one is at a disadvantage when it comes to content."
- Smaller accounts get a real chance to reach more people.

## How the circle model of distribution works

Tibo describes the algorithm as circle-by-circle distribution. He says he dug into it and shared his learnings on Twitter too.

1. **People are sorted into circles.** The platform groups every profile that has viewed or interacted with your content. Circle A holds the people most likely to engage, then circle B (engage less), then C, D and so on.
2. **Your content and profile are graded.** The better your content performs, the higher your grade. A higher grade means bigger circles.
3. **Content is distributed circle by circle.**
   - A post is shown to circle A first.
   - If circle A interacts, it goes to circle B, then C, and so on.
   - When engagement falls below a threshold, Twitter and LinkedIn stop showing the post to new people.

## Engagement beats audience size: create content that hooks

- Tibo says "Engagement is queen." Big accounts used to be read "because you were you", even when the content was boring.
- Now, if content is not engaging enough, it never reaches your own audience.
- His recommended strategy is to create content that keeps people hooked.
- That may mean content that is more surface level and covers a wider audience.
- You then funnel those people into your newsletter for a deeper conversation.
- He sees "No more posting into the void" as a consequence of this model: good, engaging content gets the eyeballs it deserves.

## Short-form video as a distribution lever

- Tibo argues video will always perform better because it retains users better. All platforms will use it to increase average time spent on the platform.
- He calls it the best time to start making short-form videos.
- One short-form video can be reused across five placements:
  - 1 Instagram reel
  - 1 YouTube short
  - 1 LinkedIn video
  - 1 Twitter video
  - 1 TikTok

## Be social to build your circle A

- Tibo: "The best way to grow on social media… is by being social."
- He says you can't win in isolation anymore.
- Engaging with people around you boosts your own metrics.
- It also brings new friends, business partners and clients.
- Those people become your "Circle A". If they love your content, they engage with it, which makes it easier to reach circle B and so gives more reach.
