## Pricing creator deals off average views, not hope

- Jake says a creator's floor has to be their average views. When he looks at a creator's page, the worst-performing videos are "almost every time" the sponsored posts, because the brand pushed the creator away from what they normally make.
- At Cal AI, every deal was priced off the creator's average views. He tells others to do the same.
- Management cost is the same regardless of creator size. A creator doing 20k views and one doing a million cost identical time: contracts, scheduling, review, comments, renewal and payment. Everyone has a bandwidth ceiling, so he spends his on the bigger creators.

## Deciding between a seamless integration and a hard sell

Two questions decide how hard the promo needs to push:

1. Is the product easy to understand?
2. Does it have a magic moment?

- If both are yes, do a seamless integration and let the product carry the promo.
- If either is missing, you need a hard sell.
- Cal AI was a yes to both. Everybody already knew what calorie tracking was, and in early 2024 "pointing a phone at a plate and getting calories in seconds stopped people in their tracks."

## Picking a niche with too many creators

- Fitness worked partly because there are far more fitness creators than brands trying to work with them.
- That supply mismatch gets you cheaper deals to start and lets you figure out which creators fit your brand.
- The demographic Jake would target now is college kids: huge supply, almost no brands that know how to work with them, "nothing but time and no money." They'll take a rate "a working professional would laugh at."

## The 20-second creator screening test

After reviewing over 10,000 creator profiles, Jake checks four things quickly:

- Do they clear 25k views a video?
- Are the comments real people sharing opinions, or just fire emojis?
- Are there reply threads under those comments?
- Do they have a parasocial relationship with their audience?

His reasoning: real comments usually mean a cult following. Reply threads mean the audience talks to each other, not just to the creator. That is a community, and "communities convert better than anything else."

## Cold outreach to creators

- Creators get around a hundred DMs a day and open maybe ten. They decide off the preview line before opening the message.
- Don't open with your life story or "I'm a bootstrapped founder." Open with "paid promo" or something that answers "What's in it for me?"
- Keep the message short enough to answer in one line.
- Whether to name a price depends on the creator. A college kid who has never done a deal sees $100 and takes it. A creator with fifty brand deals behind her will anchor against you, so let her start the money conversation.

## Pay creators 100% upfront

- Almost every experienced creator has been burned by a brand that didn't pay. That is the number one reason they say no, and paying upfront removes their biggest fear of getting ripped off.
- It also solved a personal problem for Jake. He is 30 and looks young, and he could see creators on calls thinking "this kid can't afford me." Saying he pays upfront ends that conversation before it starts.

## Find a winning format, then stop being creative

- Cal AI found one format that worked with one fitness creator, then signed as many fitness creators as possible to make the exact same video.
- While that ran, a separate team tested whether the format carried into other niches.
- His rule: "Find it, copy it, run it until it dies. Then go find the next one."

## Volume is the only unlock

- Jake says none of the tactics matter if you won't do the volume. People ask for the secret Cal AI scaling unlock, and he says there isn't one.
- He worked from 6am to midnight for two years, often falling asleep with the laptop in bed, and kept going the next morning.
- His view is that copycat Cal AI apps didn't beat Cal AI because they weren't willing to work that hard. He adds he isn't saying you have to do that, but if you're asking whether you're working hard enough, "you already know the answer."
