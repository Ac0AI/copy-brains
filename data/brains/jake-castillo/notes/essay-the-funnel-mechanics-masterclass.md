## Congruency is the core principle of a high-converting app funnel

- Jake tells almost every app founder he consults with: "Your funnel needs to be as frictionless as possible."
- The customer should feel like they are "effortlessly sliding down a slippery slope until they hit your paywall."
- The only way that happens is if every part of the funnel is congruent. His shorthand: "Congruency > everything else."
- Every screen after the first touchpoint either confirms or contradicts the story the prospect is building in their head about the solution. Confirm it enough times and they buy.

## Native ad funnels as the model of congruency

Jake calls the marketers behind native ad funnels "masters of congruency." The sequence:

1. A user scrolls a news or sports site and sees a headline that grabs attention.
2. They click something that doesn't look like an ad.
3. It leads to a health quiz or an article that teaches them about a problem they didn't know they had.
4. They spend real time (10-15 minutes) answering questions and reading about why the thing they worry about happens.
5. Only then does a product appear. By then it doesn't feel like a pitch, it feels like the answer to a problem they likely have.

- These funnels work because the story and the messaging work together seamlessly.
- The counterexample: send native ad clicks straight to a product page and you are asking cold traffic to pay for a solution to a problem they may not realize they have.

## Applying congruency to an app funnel

- Once you have identified winning angles for your product, top-of-funnel content (paid or organic) must use the same language and highlight a pain point or desired outcome.
- Every later stage must carry the same messaging all the way to the paywall: the app store page, or web2app funnel pages, then onboarding, then the paywall.
- The full path to keep consistent: content (paid or organic), store page, onboarding, paywall.

## Focus on one killer feature to simplify your angles

- Jake says this is another reason Cal AI focused on "one killer feature" rather than five mediocre ones.
- One feature simplifies the marketing angles used to promote the product.
- His framing: "Give your potential customers one idea to hold onto to survive the trip from a creator's video or ad all the way to your paywall."

## Funnel benchmarks that show where congruency breaks

Jake's diagnostic thresholds, each mapping a weak metric to the two stages that disagree:

| Benchmark | What it means |
|---|---|
| Under 5 downloads per 1,000 views | Your creative and your store page disagree |
| Under 75% paywall rate | Your store page and your onboarding disagree |
| Under 10% paywall conversion | Your onboarding and your offer disagree |

- His conclusion: "stop blindly optimizing steps and start auditing every step of your funnel."

## The funnel audit exercise

- Run through your own funnel "like a stranger" in one sitting this week: content (paid or organic), store page, onboarding, paywall.
- If it doesn't flow seamlessly end to end, find the fixes and watch what happens to your dashboard.
