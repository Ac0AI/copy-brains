## The three-stage diagnostic: views, downloads, paywall

Jake argues that most founders who say "my app isn't growing" cannot name the numbers that show why. He says he asks every founder whether they know four metrics, and "very few" can recite all four:

1. Views generated by content and ads
2. Impressions on the App Store page
3. Paywall rate: the % of downloads that actually reach the paywall
4. Paywall conversion rate

Once you have these numbers, he says, "you know exactly which lever to pull." Two questions explain every one of the 300+ influencer deals he signed on the way to $50M ARR at Cal AI:

- Did the views turn into downloads?
- Did the downloads turn into paying users?

He says follower counts, likes and how good the video looked "never made the list."

## Cal AI benchmark targets

Jake lists the targets his team aimed for at Cal AI:

- **View to download:** 5 downloads per 1,000 views (0.5%)
- **Paywall rate:** 75%+ of downloads reach the paywall
- **Paywall conversion:** 10%

His claim: hit these three consistently and "you will not have a user acquisition problem."

## Diagnosing which problem you actually have

Jake maps each weak metric to one kind of problem, so you know what to fix before spending money:

- **Views are low:** a traffic problem. Get more eyeballs. Post more, sign more creators, or run ads if you have the capital.
- **Views are high but downloads are weak:** a message problem, or a demand problem. People saw the content and didn't care. Either the hook is wrong or nobody wants what you are selling.
- **Downloads are strong but conversion is weak:** a product problem. Look at onboarding and the paywall first, and see where people drop off in the flow.

## Fixing the wrong thing

Jake says founders "love fixing the wrong thing." Two examples he has seen:

- People rebuilding a paywall when the real issue was that they needed more traffic.
- People pouring money into creators when half their users never made it to the paywall.

He says both feel productive but neither changes revenue. This is why he wants the numbers pulled before anyone spends on a fix.

## Keep the measurement stack simple

Jake warns founders to be wary of anyone who makes this sound complicated, such as "a full attribution stack, a 40-slide audit, and a 90-day retainer." In his view that is usually someone trying to sell a service. He says you can usually start with the four metrics above and resolve the issue.

## What to do this week

Jake's instruction for a struggling app:

- Pull the four numbers this week.
- Whichever one is furthest off from the targets is your job for the next month.
- Everything else waits.
