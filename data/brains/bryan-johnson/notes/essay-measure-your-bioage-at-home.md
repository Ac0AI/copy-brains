## Free at-home Bioage Fitness tests

Bryan's Blueprint clinical team designed seven free fitness tests that estimate biological age without lab work. Results are entered in the Don't Die App to produce a biological age.

**How to log results:**
1. Download the Don't Die App.
2. Open the profile and go to "Measurements".
3. Click the + next to "Capabilities".
4. Select the fitness assessment being completed (instructions are in the app).
5. Enter the results to receive a biological age.

## Strength and endurance tests: push-ups and grip strength

**Continuous push-ups** measure upper body strength and endurance. The goal is as many push-ups as possible without rest.
- Start face down, hands slightly wider than shoulder-width, elbows fully extended.
- Lower until the chest touches the floor, body in a straight line from head to heels. Don't round the back.
- Push back up; that is one rep.
- "Without rest" means not holding at the top for 3 seconds.
- Record the total completed with proper form.

**Grip strength** measures hand and forearm strength and correlates with cardiovascular disease risk. Larger numbers are better.
- Use a grip strength dynamometer.
- Stand upright, holding it at your side, and squeeze as hard as possible.
- Do 3 attempts per hand and record the highest reading in lb.

## Mobility and flexibility tests: Sit Rise Test and Sit and Reach

**Sit Rise Test** assesses flexibility, strength and balance.
- From standing, sit on the floor cross-legged, then rise back to standing, without using hands, knees, forearms or the side of the legs for support.
- Scoring: deduct 1 point for each hand, knee or forearm used going down and up.
- Maximum score is 10; 8 or more is considered ideal.

**Sit and Reach (YMCA)** measures flexibility of the lower back and hamstrings. Reaching further is better.
- Sit with legs straight out in front, feet about 10-12 inches apart.
- Lay one tape measure along the floor and place a second at the 15" mark, crossing the first (~5-6 inch mark) to form a "T" or "+", with zero closest to you. Heels rest on the first tape measure.
- Stack one hand on the other, palms down, and reach forward as far as possible along the measuring line.
- Hold the stretch for 1-2 seconds and measure the distance reached by the fingertips.
- Repeat twice and record the best distance.

## Body composition and balance: waist-to-height ratio and one-leg stand

**Waist-to-height ratio** assesses body fat distribution, metabolic health and risk. A smaller ratio is better.
- Measure height in cm.
- Measure waist circumference in cm at the narrowest point between the ribs and hips.
- Ratio = waist circumference (cm) / height (cm).

**One-leg stand / balance test** measures balance and lower body strength. Longer is better.
- Stand on one leg with eyes shut, the other leg bent at the knee, lifted and free floating.
- Hold as long as possible without touching the raised leg to the ground, using external support, or touching the other leg.
- Record the time in seconds, and test both legs.

## Reaction time test

This measures quickness of response to a stimulus. A smaller reaction time is better.
- Download the app "Reaction Time & Reflex Test" and open the game "Lights Out".
- Start a new test and place a thumb or finger on the screen to start the countdown; all 5 lights illuminate.
- Lift the finger or thumb as soon as the lights go out.
- Repeat 5 times and take the best score, which is the lowest number.
