## Bryan's haircare outcome and starting early

- Bryan says his hair is better at 49 than at 29. He started losing hair and going gray in his late 20s. In his late 40s he reports a full head of hair and about 70% of his gray gone.
- He frames this as personal experience ("Results vary"), not a guarantee.
- He advises starting a hair protocol before thinning is visible.
  - By age 20, about 20% of men already have some visible hair loss.
  - Many people lose up to 50% of their hair before they notice it.
  - By age 50, up to 40% of women have noticeable hair loss.
- His basics: good nutrition, science-backed haircare protocols, and starting treatments early.

## Nutrition for hair

- Bryan holds that the body needs good food to make good hair. He eats whole foods and avoids processed junk and added sugar.
- Nutrients he prioritizes for hair:
  - protein
  - omega-3
  - iron, selenium, biotin, zinc, and vitamins C, D, and E
- His sources: fruits, vegetables, lentils, nuts, seeds, Longevity Mix, and Essential Capsules.
- He also supplements with high-potency omega-3 and broad-spectrum collagen peptides.
- For people who eat meat, he recommends chicken, turkey, and fatty fish such as salmon or mackerel.

## Minoxidil: oral and topical

- **Oral minoxidil:** He started at 2.5 mg and evaluated for side effects. He now takes 3.75 mg a day. He notes it is commonly used to support hair regrowth.
- **Topical minoxidil:** He applies 1 mL of 5% minoxidil to the scalp each night.
- He notes oral minoxidil is a prescription medication. It should be used only under the supervision of a qualified healthcare provider, may cause side effects, and is not appropriate for everyone.
