## Measuring all 70+ organs and reversing biological age (Project Blueprint)

- Project Blueprint aims to measure all 70+ organs and then maximally reverse the quantified biological age of each.
- As of October 2021, over 15 organs were measured and Bryan had scored 507 age reversal points.
- His chronological age was 44 and his measured biological age was 36.
- He calls Blueprint "a stock ticker of sorts" that reveals the status of anti-aging science through tracking biological versus chronological age (an N=1 for now).
- Aging escape velocity: reversing measured biological age by 1.01 years for every year that passes would be evidence that life expectancy increases faster than time passes.
- On a magic pill, he says that for the foreseeable future, "achieving and maintaining peak wellness is just going to be a lot of really hard work."
- Blueprint is not Quantified Self or Life Optimization. He frames it as "an exploration into the future of being human."

## Evidence standards and how interventions are chosen

- For every measurement and intervention, the team finds literature that establishes an age reference range.
- They then identify gold-standard randomized controlled trial (RCT) evidence to reverse that age marker, and begin trialing interventions.
- They partner with local practitioners and custom compounded pharmacies, and iterate on novel approaches.
- Rules he states: "we never act on anecdotes," never do something because others do it, and use evidence-based medicine only.
- He "trusts his gut" only through its data (biomarkers), not through subjective emotional states.
- The team publicly journals its efforts monthly, like Lewis & Clark and Shackleton chronicled expeditions.
- He says the system matters more than the specific diet: Blueprint is "a time-consuming, tedious, costly, experimental evidence-based process to create systems that automatically self improve over time." The goal is for it to become transferable so anyone can find the best diet for their body.

## Firing "Evening Bryan" and the Autonomous Self

- The project began when he fired "Evening Bryan" from authority over what and how much to eat: no late-night binges, no second servings, no "just-this-once" rationalizations.
- His body now "generates the grocery shopping list" through regular measurement of hundreds of biomarkers.
- This gives the heart, liver, kidneys and other organs an independent voice whose authority surpasses his conscious mind. He calls this his Autonomous Self.
- He describes demoting his conscious mind as one of the most positive developments of his life.
- Broader aim: free people from daily health management so they can focus on building the future. He cites A.N. Whitehead: "Civilization advances by extending the number of important operations which we can perform without thinking of them."

## Heart rate variability and oral age gains

- HRV rose to 54 ms from 37 ms in five months (Whoop, 14-day average rMSSD, deep sleep, 5 minutes).
- He equates this to reducing the sympathetic nervous system vagal tone biomarker age from 54 to 39.6.
- Oral age (pocket depth) was reversed by 17 years in 120 days without surgical intervention.
- In September the oral age progress plateaued, and the team began reassessing the next phase.

## Mistakes and corrections: body fat and measurement errors

- A one-meal-a-day experiment (chosen for convenience, not devised by his team) dropped body fat below 3%, which he says was too low.
- He restored body fat to 6% (optimal range) by moving to three meals a day plus a 10-16 hour daily fast.
- The team made an error measuring skin age via Skin Autofluorescence because the measurement protocol was not followed properly.
- He says the team frequently makes mistakes and stumbles onto new findings.
