## Sleep is the foundation: build life around it

- Bryan ranks sleep as the number one lever, "by far". Good sleep restores willpower; poor sleep "knocks your prefrontal cortex offline" and he says it destroys willpower.
- His order of operations: master sleep, then exercise (also boosts willpower), then tackle food last, since food is where people go to self-soothe.
- He says that if you're not well rested, well nourished and exercising, your functional IQ is 10 to 12 points lower.
- He says regularity matters more than anything: inconsistent sleep is as bad as little sleep, and you can't skip during the week and "make it up" on the weekend.
- His mental model: a "garbage truck" (cleansing/lymphatic system) rolls through around 10:30 p.m. if you're asleep in deep sleep. Miss it and the trash builds up.
- He says to pick your own archetype (regimented or flexible), but the body's circadian rules can't be overridden. A three-hour swing in bedtimes across a week doesn't work.

## Bryan's sleep protocol and resting heart rate

- Core metric: lower your resting heart rate before bed. He calls it the highest-value biomarker to track daily. Everything that raises it before bed (except sex) is bad; everything that lowers it is good.
- Last meal 4 hours before bed (10 pm bedtime means a 6 pm meal). He personally leaves 10 to 12 hours, which brings his heart rate to about 39 to 40 bpm.
- Screens off 60 minutes before bed, a hard cut. Avoid scrolling, texting and working.
- Red or amber light at home; avoid white and blue light.
- Last caffeine around noon (about 6-hour half-life).
- Use the 60-minute wind-down to "be with yourself" without stimulation: walk, breathwork, meditation, puzzle, journaling, time with a friend or family.
- Target: about 50 bpm resting heart rate for men and women, lower is better.
- His own bedtime is 8:30 p.m., and "sleep Bryan" comes on duty at 7:30 p.m.
- Supplements: only about 300 mcg of melatonin, to offset pineal calcification with age. He says people overdo it with 5 to 50 mg. He prefers building habits over pills.

## Taming the internal voices

- Before bed he talks to parts of himself, e.g. "ambitious Bryan" (new ideas) and "anxious Bryan" (reviewing the day's mistakes).
- He writes down ambitious Bryan's idea for tomorrow and thanks him. Without this reconciliation, they show up at 2 a.m.
- Don't let the routine become a source of anxiety: trying harder at sleep can backfire.

## Nighttime erections as a health biomarker

- Men and women have 3 to 5 arousal cycles a night (erections; clitoral engorgement). He says they depend on sleep quality, metabolic, cardiovascular and hormone health.
- He calls nighttime erections one of the most representative overall-health biomarkers, one you can't fake or "work out"; it either happens or it doesn't.
- His record: 3 hours 49 minutes in a night. He puts an elite 18-year-old around 2 hours 45 minutes. The device company's data set is about 20,000 nights, and he says his scores beat everyone.
- Framing: four hours of sleep means no erections, which makes sleep deprivation low status instead of a flex.

## Behavior change: fire the bad version of yourself

- Do less. He says most things in health don't work; pick a few. The biggest yield is usually stopping a bad behavior, not adding red light or cold plunges to compensate.
- His method: he overate every evening around 7 p.m. for years. He named the voice "evening Bryan" and "fired" him: no food from 5 p.m. to 10 p.m., no exceptions.
- He wrote down evening Bryan's arguments ("tonight's the last night", "I'll work out hard tomorrow") and recognizes them when they appear.
- Rule: "none is better than some." No moderation, no cheat meals, days or weeks. He says "everything in moderation" is not done in moderation.
- The core principle is agency: "Don't be puppeted." Treat compulsive scrolling and fast food as an enemy to overcome, which gives moral energy.
- Slow-walk food changes: add more good things and a few less bad ones.

## Exercise and food (minimal prescriptions)

- Exercise: be active roughly an hour a day, mixing cardio, strength, balance and flexibility. He doesn't care which style.
- Food: he avoids carnivore vs vegan debates. Eat what you want, measure your body, fine-tune. He points to his Don't Die food guide (highest-evidence foods).
- Testosterone: he's naturally in the 700s from basics. He says four hours of sleep can cut testosterone about 10 to 20%. If a man is low and natural approaches fail, he thinks medical help makes sense, but he isn't confident in supplements and says his knowledge is limited.
- Methylene blue: he tried 25 mg a day but stopped (it conflicted with altitude training) and doesn't think it's worth it.

## Sauna protocol and results

- Dry sauna only: infrared isn't hot enough, wet sauna is too hot to tolerate. 20 minutes a day at 200 °F (93 °C), after a one-hour workout, so he's already warm.
- Wipe sweat with a cotton towel, then shower right after.
- Ice packs on the testicles during sauna (cheap BPA-free packs from Amazon, about $8 for four), between two layers of cotton underwear and shorts. Without ice, his fertility markers fell about 50% in two weeks.
- After 3 weeks with ice he saw about a 30% jump in markers; after 6 weeks back on ice, his highest ever sperm count, motility and morphology (he says 6 to 10 times typical levels, from memory). He doesn't know the mechanism.
- Results he reports: toxins from the LA wildfires cleared; microplastics down over 90% in blood and semen (he says a first-in-world demonstration); VEGF up 400%; pTau217 (Alzheimer's marker) dropped.
- Adaptation: sauna initially wrecked his sleep for about 2 weeks, in the morning too. After adapting, evening sauna improved sleep. Test it yourself. Avoid exercising within 4 hours of bed.

## Hyperbaric oxygen (HBOT) and skin

- He calls HBOT the best-performing therapy he's ever done, but expensive, time-consuming and inaccessible, so he stresses people don't need it.
- His protocol: 60 sessions over 90 days, then about 200 sessions over a year. Mask on 20 minutes, off 5 minutes. He takes sprints followed by week-long breaks to avoid oxygen toxicity.
- Results across the board: pTau217 dropped, zero dysbiosis in his microbiome, no detectable inflammation (hsCRP zero).
- Other uses he cites: cognitive decline, diabetic wound healing, post-surgery recovery.
- Skin: he calls it the best skin rejuvenation therapy, remodeling collagen, elastin and reducing senescent cells. His stack: a ~1550 nm laser for surface (spots, wrinkles, texture), SoftWave ultrasound for the deeper dermis, then HBOT to speed healing.
