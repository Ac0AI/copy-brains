## Why the final meal is at noon

Bryan's single most impactful sleep change was moving his final meal to around noon, roughly 8.5 hours before bed.

- When he eats close to bed, his resting heart rate before sleep climbs to 52-56 bpm and he loses roughly 30% of his sleep quality.
- When his heart rate is 39 to 44 bpm, he says his sleep is perfect.
- His reasoning: the body struggles to enter deep sleep while digesting, because it is being asked to do two conflicting things, digest and sleep.
- He says you don't need a noon cutoff. Start by finishing food 2 hours before bed, then move to 3 hours, then 4. Each step shows up in your data the next morning.
- His advice: "Experiment and find what's right for you."

## Results and where to start

- Bryan says he is asleep within 3 min of his head hitting the pillow, every night.
- His sleep performance ranks in the 99.4th percentile, which he says took 5 years to build systems for.
- His routine changes as his team updates his protocols.
- He says you don't need to copy his routine or overhaul everything. Start small, with turning off screens 60 min before bed.

## HRV therapy and breathing

- Bryan describes HRV as the variability between heartbeats, with higher HRV reflecting a more resilient nervous system.
- Before bed he does 10 min of 4:6 breathing: in through the nose for 4 seconds, out for 6.
- This activates the parasympathetic nervous system, elevating HRV and calming him for sleep.

## Skin, hair and oral hygiene (done before winding down)

He does these before the wind-down so that when it's time to sleep he can go straight to bed.

- **Skincare:** Blueprint Gentle Facial Cleanser, SFC Facial Serum and SFC Facial Moisturizer, plus tretinoin nightly.
- **Haircare:** a custom 1 mL topical 5% minoxidil solution applied to the scalp each night. In the morning he applies Blueprint Peptide Hair Serum and takes oral minoxidil.
- **Oral hygiene:** Waterpik, floss, brush teeth, tongue scraper.
- **Bruxism:** he sleeps with a bruxism device (SomnoDent Avant) to prevent teeth grinding.

## Melatonin and lighting

- **Melatonin:** he takes 300 mcg each night and nothing else for sleep.
- **Lighting:** the only lights in his house are 2 low cost lamps, one amber and one red. Otherwise he cycles his daily routines with the sun.
- His rationale: modern lighting is harsh, and blue light reduces production of the sleep hormone melatonin. Red light helps you unwind.

## 60-min wind-down and "Sleep Bryan"

- He turns off screens 60 min before bed and enables "Sleep Bryan."
- Sleep Bryan's job is to get his mind and body ready for sleep. When thoughts arise about everything still to be done, he reminds all the other Bryans that "we have all day tomorrow" to take care of these things.
- During this hour he usually hangs out with family, walks and reads.
- He likes a real book in hand 10-20 min before sleep, because it creates separation from devices.
- His tip: set an alarm if you need a reminder to wind down.

## Consistent bedtime

- Bryan gets into bed at 8:30 pm and is usually asleep within 3 min.
- He says you don't need to go to bed this early, but you should be consistent.
- His rule: choose a time that allows 7-9 hours of sleep, and go to bed within +/- 30 min of that time every night.
