## Bedtime and sleep as the foundation

- Bryan is in bed by 8:30 pm and wakes naturally around 5 am.
- He calls sleep his "#1 life priority" because "high-quality rest makes hard things easy and impossible things doable."
- He says every day starts the night before.
- His full daily routine currently takes 4 hours 46 min. It used to take longer. He and his team scrutinized every step and dramatically simplified it from the more complicated early Blueprint version.

## Wake-up sequence: oral hygiene, light, and breath work

1. **Oral hygiene (first thing):** water pik, floss, brush, and a tongue scraper.
2. **Light in eyes:** he wakes before sunrise, so he uses a 10,000 lux light for 6 min to simulate natural morning light and support his circadian rhythm (body clock).
3. **Hair health:** he wears the Blueprint 302 Laser Cap for 6 min, during the light session.
4. **Breath work:** a breathing exercise or meditation for 6 min "to get my mind and body right for the day."

## Exercise: 90 minutes daily

- The workout mixes cardio, strength training, balance and stretching, and high-intensity interval training (2x per week).
- He rotates between listening to music, a book, a podcast, or an educational video.

## Mindset: simplify, start small, let it compound

- He says the routine is "always evolving" as protocols are updated, and this is what he is experimenting with now.
- He does not expect anyone to follow his routine.
- For people who are struggling: "start small. A short walk. 5 min of meditation. 1 better meal. Then let the improvements compound."
- He says he spent nearly 20 years wrecking his body, and argues that if he can reverse that, others can see what might be possible for them.
