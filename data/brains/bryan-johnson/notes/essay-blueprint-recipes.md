## Vegetable stir fry with cauliflower rice

Bryan lists this as one of the recipes he eats regularly, calling eating "one of life's greatest joys." Quantities are by weight:

- Vegetables: 1/2 medium onion (about 75 g), 1/4 red cabbage and 1/4 white cabbage (about 150 g each), 200 g mung bean sprouts, 200 g broccoli, 225 g shiitake mushrooms, 150 g sugar snap peas.
- Seasoning: 15 g ginger powder, 15 g garlic powder, 30 ml gluten-free low-sodium tamari.
- Base: 600 g cauliflower rice, cooked in 15 ml olive oil.
- Optional garnish: 7 g hemp seeds.

## Soups: lemon red lentil and butternut squash

**Lemon red lentil soup**
- 15-30 ml olive oil, 3 large carrots (about 360 g), 2 celery stalks (about 180 g), 1 yellow onion (about 150 g), 4 cloves garlic.
- 1.5 liters vegetable stock, 225 g red lentils, 2 bay leaves.
- 2.5 g turmeric, 5 g cumin, juice of 1 lemon, optional lemon zest.

**Butternut squash soup with roasted apples and carrots (serves 4-6)**
- 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves, 1 butternut squash (about 4 cups cubed), 2 Honeycrisp apples, 2 large carrots.
- 1 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp ginger powder.
- 4 cups vegetable broth, 1 cup coconut milk, 2 tbsp olive oil.

## Chickpea-based dishes: frittata, stew and chickpea rice bowl

**Chickpea vegetable frittata**
- 240 g chickpea flour and 360 ml water form the egg-free base.
- 1/2 onion, 2 red peppers, 1 zucchini, 1 bunch each of parsley and chives, 2 garlic cloves, 1 Roma tomato, 225 g mushrooms.
- 15 g garlic powder, 15 g ground cumin, 15 ml olive oil.

**Chickpea stew**
- 5 garlic cloves, 2 tbsp (30 mL) extra virgin olive oil, 2 tbsp (30 g) tomato paste.
- Spices: 1 tbsp (6-8 g) cumin, 1 tbsp (6-8 g) paprika, 1 tsp (3 g) red pepper flakes, 2-3 tsp (5-7 g) za'atar, 2-3 bay leaves.
- 3 cups (500 g) cooked chickpeas, 1 roasted bell pepper.

**Black bean and mushroom bowl with chickpea rice (serves 2-3)**
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, 1 cup cherry tomatoes, 1/2 onion, 1 cup oyster mushrooms.
- Seasoning: 1 tsp umami seasoning, 1/2 tsp cumin, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, 1 tbsp olive oil.
- Toppings: 1/4 cup pickled red onions, 1 cup spinach, 1/2 cup shredded carrots, 1/2 cup sliced radish, 1/4 cup cilantro.
- Served over 1 1/2 cups cooked chickpea rice.

## Plant-based "meat" dishes: lentil loaf, walnut bolognese and cauliflower "turkey"

**Cauliflower lentil loaf**
- Loaf: 200 g cooked lentils, 300 g riced cauliflower, 150 g onion, 8 oz mushrooms, 30 g nutritional yeast.
- Seasoning: 5 g minced garlic, 5 g Dijon mustard, 2.5 g smoked paprika, 2.5 g cumin, 1.5 g black pepper, 1.5 g chipotle powder.
- Glaze: 80 ml unsweetened ketchup and 5 ml liquid smoke.

**Mushroom and walnut bolognese with spaghetti squash**
- 225 g cremini mushrooms, 40 g each of onion, carrot and celery, 2 garlic cloves, 60 g walnuts.
- 15 g garlic powder, 15 g Italian seasoning, 15 g tomato paste, 340 g tomato sauce.
- 30 ml gluten-free tamari, 30 ml balsamic vinegar.
- Served on a roasted medium spaghetti squash (about 1/2 squash), with 5 ml olive oil for greasing.

**Herb-seasoned cauliflower "turkey" with sweet potato mash and green beans**
- "Turkey": 1 large cauliflower head, 30 ml olive oil, 10 g each of garlic powder, onion powder and smoked paprika, 5 g each of dried thyme and rosemary, 15 g nutritional yeast, 100 ml vegetable broth for basting.
- Sweet potato mash: 800 g sweet potatoes, 30-60 ml plant milk, 5 g garlic powder, 5 g cinnamon.
- Green beans: 400 g, blanched in ice water.

## Squash, lentil and curry dishes

**Mushroom walnut stuffed acorn squash**
- 2 medium acorn squash, halved, with 1 tbsp olive oil.
- Filling: 1 tbsp olive oil, 16 oz crimini mushrooms, 4 tbsp walnuts, 4 tbsp unsweetened dried cranberries, 1 tbsp honey, 1/2 tbsp cinnamon.

**Black lentil and olive salad with spiced roasted carrots and sun-dried tomato hummus**
- Carrots: 3 large carrots, 1 shallot, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp maple syrup, 1 tsp each of smoked paprika and cumin, 1/2 tsp salt.
- Salad: 1 cup black lentils cooked in 2 cups water or broth, 1 tbsp red wine vinegar, 1 tsp Dijon, 1/4 cup black currants, 1/2 cup Castelvetrano olives, 1/3 cup roasted almonds, 1/4 cup parsley, 2 tbsp mint.
- Served with 1/2 cup sun-dried tomato hummus.

**Sweet potato and cabbage curry with cauliflower rice**
- 2 cups shredded cabbage, 1 small/medium sweet potato (1-inch pieces), 1 onion, 2 tbsp olive oil.
- Spices: 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 dry red chili, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp coriander, 1/2 tsp red chili powder, 1/2 tsp salt.
- Finish with 1/4 cup cilantro and 1 tbsp lime juice, served over 2 cups cauliflower rice.

## Breakfast and snack: grain-free porridge and superfood smoothie

**Grain-free porridge with butterfly pea flower**
- 250 ml macadamia nut milk, 30 g almond flour, 15 g hemp seeds, 10 g chia seeds, 10 g ground flax.
- 1/2 tsp vanilla, 1 tsp butterfly pea flower powder, 1/4 tsp cardamom.
- Blend until smooth and top with a handful of fresh berries.

**Superfood smoothie (serves 1-2)**
- 1/2 cup each of strawberries, blueberries and pitted dark cherries, 1/4 cup pomegranate arils.
- 1 cup almond milk, 1 tbsp flax seeds, 5-6 macadamia nuts, 1 tsp chia seeds, 1 tsp Blueprint cacao.
- Blend until smooth.

## Patterns across Bryan's recipes

- Every recipe is plant-based and built on vegetables, legumes, mushrooms, nuts and seeds.
- Cauliflower rice, spaghetti squash and chickpea rice stand in for grains and pasta.
- Olive oil is the main fat, with garlic, cumin, smoked paprika and turmeric as staple seasonings.
- Sugar is minimal. Ketchup is unsweetened, cranberries are unsweetened, and sweeteners appear only in small amounts (1 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp maple syrup).
- Ingredients are weighed in grams, in line with his measured, protocol-driven approach.
