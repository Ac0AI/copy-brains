## Skin age results and the baseline damage Bryan started from

- Until age 40, Bryan says he "kind of destroyed" his skin: sunburns, poor diet, no skincare routine.
- When he started Blueprint, his skin was in the 98th percentile for damage on some markers, with an average skin age of 64.
- His skin age has since improved to between 37 and 42.
- He tracks progress by routinely measuring skin biological age with multispectral imaging (Visia is a popular US choice). It reports UV damage, pore size, dark spots and other indicators. Some wellness clinics offer it.

## Skin basics: lifestyle foundations

- Exercise.
- Prioritize sleep.
- Eat a healthy, Blueprint-like diet.
- Avoid junk, fried or highly processed foods, cigarettes and vaping.
- He says to get sun earlier in the day and later in the evening, when the UV index is lower.
- Position on sun: "The sun is good for you but not too much and not too little. Just the right amount will do."
- If in direct sun when the UV index is high (10 am to 4 pm), protect with clothing, a UV umbrella, a hat and mineral sunscreen.
- He says that if you lack access to extras like red light therapy, don't worry; the basics are a great place to start.

## Daily skincare routine

- Face wash morning and night.
- Mineral sunscreen.
- Moisturize body and face.
- Targeted active ingredients:
  - Niacinamide, morning and night, in serums or moisturizers.
  - Vitamin C, morning, typically in a serum.
  - Hyaluronic acid, as desired, usually in a serum or moisturizer.
  - Tretinoin (prescription), at night.
- He advises spotting endocrine disruptors in skincare products: chemicals that may mimic or interfere with the body's hormones, linked with health problems in wildlife and humans.

## Pro tips for UV and blemish control

- UV-tinted windows to block 99% of damaging UV rays.
- A UV umbrella during high UV index hours.
- Microdosing Accutane at 40 mg weekly for blemish-free skin. Over-the-counter retinols are an alternative.
- Red light therapy 3x per week.
