## Dustin's criteria for a good affiliate tracking platform

Dustin frames affiliate tracking software as the fix for manual tracking of clicks, leads and commissions across spreadsheets. It assigns unique tracking codes or cookies so each conversion is attributed to the right affiliate. He lists five benefits:

- Accurate tracking that prevents revenue leakage.
- Transparency, so both sides trust the data for fair payouts.
- Time savings from automated commission calculations and reporting.
- Performance insights that identify top affiliates.
- Fraud prevention, such as catching invalid clicks.

A good platform, in his view, does these things:

- Tracks clicks, conversions and other metrics accurately, with real-time analytics.
- Is easy to use for both businesses and affiliates.
- Allows customization of tracking links, commissions and reports.
- Has built-in fraud detection.
- Integrates with CRMs and e-commerce platforms.
- Is not just about management; "it should empower growth and drive success."

## Best picks for SaaS and B2B: Everflow, PartnerStack, Inflektion

**Everflow** is his "best for overall affiliate tracking" pick. It works with any vertical (software, B2B, e-commerce), is cheaper than the big networks, and he calls it the biggest up-and-comer in the industry.

- Pricing starts at $750/month with a 6-month commitment.
- Pros: intuitive, robust tracking and reporting, quick support, mobile management.
- Cons: initially complex, occasional bugs and API issues, campaigns with broken links need manual pausing.
- In 2025 Everflow partnered with Skimlinks for a full transparency integration, giving brands a picture of sub-affiliate network traffic.

**PartnerStack** is the platform he says currently leads the industry for SaaS partnerships and affiliate programs.

- Strengths: easy partner onboarding, advanced analytics, automation, customizable programs, integrated payouts.
- Weaknesses: learning curve, fewer integrations than some competitors, cost for smaller businesses.
- Pricing is not public; it is quote-based.

**Inflektion** is his pick for high conversion rates and health and wellness.

- It makes co-branded, personalized landing pages and funnels that drive high conversion, especially for unique products influencers like.
- Integration takes under two weeks, and pricing starts at $495/month.
- Cons: needs regular updates, complex setups may need outside support, and the free trial may feel too short.

## Best picks for e-commerce and coupon programs: ShareASale/Awin and Refersion

**ShareASale/Awin** is his "best for SMB eCommerce" pick.

- It integrates with Shopify, WooCommerce and Wix, and connects merchants to over 1 million pre-screened affiliates.
- Pricing: a one-time non-refundable $625 setup fee, a $125 minimum deposit, a $35 monthly platform fee starting one month after launch, and a 20% tracking fee on commissions for qualified transactions.
- Cons: learning curve, premium features cost more, and a platform transition planned for 2025.

**Refersion** is his "best for coupon strategy" pick because it creates vanity codes for partners, which keeps coupon-driven sales trackable.

- It integrates with Shopify, WooCommerce and BigCommerce, automates affiliate payments, offers first-party and multi-domain tracking, and complies with GDPR and CCPA.
- Pricing (annual / monthly billing): Starter $79 / $99 (up to 10 monthly conversions), Professional $99 / $119 (50 conversions), Business $249 / $299 (200 conversions), Custom for unlimited conversions. There is a 14-day free trial.
- Cons: cost for smaller businesses, limited customization.

## Best picks for large programs and mobile: Impact and TUNE

**Impact** is his "best for big programs" pick, trusted by brands like Walmart and Nike.

- It manages affiliates, influencers and referrals in one platform, automates recruitment, contracts, tracking and payment, and includes fraud detection.
- Dustin says it is "not recommended for SMBs with less than $10 million in revenue."
- Pricing is not public (sales quote); a free trial is available.
- Cons: complex setup, higher cost, steep learning curve.

**TUNE** is his "best for mobile tracking" pick.

- It offers native postback tracking across apps and web, an API with over 650 endpoints, white-labeling, real-time data streaming, SOC 2 Type II certification and GDPR compliance.
- Cons: complexity can overwhelm small teams, steep pricing for small businesses, limited support for custom needs.
- Pricing is quote-based.

## Best budget option: FirstPromoter

**FirstPromoter** is his "best for companies on a budget" pick and the most budget-friendly on his list.

- It integrates with Stripe, Paddle and Recurly, reports 18 key data points, offers customizable branded affiliate dashboards, automated email, and one-click auto-payouts.
- Pros: easy setup, strong reporting.
- Cons: limited e-commerce features, the Starter plan restricts campaigns and affiliates, no support for one-time purchase models, and the interface may feel basic.
- All plans include a 14-day free trial.

## How Dustin suggests choosing

His takeaway is to pick the tool that fits your workflow and stage rather than guess at attribution:

- SaaS: Everflow or PartnerStack.
- Small e-commerce: ShareASale/Awin.
- Coupon-heavy programs: Refersion.
- Enterprise-scale programs: Impact.
- Mobile apps: TUNE.
- Influencer-heavy health and wellness: Inflektion.
- Tight budgets: FirstPromoter.

His closing point is that "every click and commission matters," so a proper tracking system is "non-negotiable."
