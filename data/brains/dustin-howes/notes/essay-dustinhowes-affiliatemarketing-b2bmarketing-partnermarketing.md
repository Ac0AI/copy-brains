## Speed to lead matters in affiliate recruitment

- When an affiliate replies "yes" to a recruitment email and asks whether they accept paid placements, Dustin says to "get on that call. No questions asked."
- He calls speed to lead "everything" in affiliate recruitment.
- He cites a Harvard Business Review study: responding within 60 seconds increases sales by almost 400%. He says the same applies to affiliates.
- Waiting a day or two loses the interest: "They've moved on with their life."

## Measure influence and conversions together

- Dustin says that without the ability to measure influence and conversions at the same time, "you're dead."
- Last-touch attribution "will lie to you."
- Example: one brand killed its Facebook ads because conversions looked low, and sales then dropped 25%. The brand had failed to understand what that touchpoint was doing in the buying journey.

## Coach partners who promote but don't convert

- If a partner is promoting but not converting, Dustin says to hop on a call and see where they're getting stuck.
- Give them the angles that work.
- His framing: affiliate is "a relationship business."
