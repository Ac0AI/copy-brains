## Black Friday has gone international

- Black Friday is no longer just an American shopping holiday.
- Expats and internationally minded shoppers across Asia plan their purchases around it.
- For many US brands, these are sales they are currently missing.

## Reaching Asian shoppers without changing fulfillment

- With Q4 approaching, Dustin asked Jeff Unze of Buy&Ship how brands can reach these shoppers without changing how they ship.
- Per Dustin's summary, the answer comes down to two things: a commission and a whitelisted address.
- The approach lets a US brand keep its existing shipping setup while still serving international buyers.

## Positioning on "we don't ship there"

- Dustin's framing (from the post's URL slug) is that "we don't ship there" is costing brands more than they think.
- The lost revenue comes from shoppers who want the product but are blocked by shipping restrictions.
- He treats cross-border affiliate and forwarding partners as a way to capture that demand.

## Channels and tags

- Dustin files this under affiliate marketing, e-commerce, and cross-border e-commerce.
