## Affiliate software for subscription businesses must follow the billing lifecycle

- Dustin argues that affiliate tracking for subscription businesses is not "click, sale, commission."
- A customer might sign up on a $49 plan, upgrade to $99 two months later, apply a discount code, then churn for a billing cycle and come back.
- A generic tool built for one-time ecommerce purchases has no model for any of that.
- The result is overpaying affiliates on canceled accounts or underpaying them on upgrades that were never tracked.
- That is why he treats subscription-specific affiliate software as its own category.
- These platforms sync to the billing provider (Stripe, Chargebee, Paddle, Recurly, Braintree) so commissions follow renewals, upgrades, downgrades, refunds and cancellations automatically, instead of being reconciled in a spreadsheet each month.
- Ecommerce tools track a single purchase event. Subscription tools need a direct billing-provider integration, not a one-time "sale" webhook.
- Without billing sync, he says you typically track the initial sale only and adjust everything afterward by hand.

## Checklist for choosing subscription affiliate software

Dustin lists what to confirm before picking a tool on price alone:

- Native billing-provider sync (Stripe, Chargebee, Paddle, Recurly, Braintree), not just a purchase webhook.
- Lifecycle-aware commissions that adjust on upgrades, downgrades and cancellations.
- Self-serve setup, so you don't sit through a sales demo just to see pricing.
- Transparent pricing tied to affiliate-driven revenue, rather than vague "contact us" tiers (unless you are already enterprise scale).
- Built-in fraud protection, since subscription businesses are a common target for self-referral abuse.
- Room to add referral and influencer programs later without switching platforms.

Budget guidance: most self-serve subscription tools start around $49-$99/month for programs generating up to $15,000/month in affiliate-driven revenue, then move to custom Enterprise pricing. Full B2B partner platforms and enterprise tools (PartnerStack, Everflow) are quote-based and typically cost more.

## FirstPromoter: his top pick for subscription businesses

- Dustin ranks FirstPromoter best overall because it is built specifically for subscription businesses, not adapted for them.
- It syncs to Stripe, Paddle, Recurly, Chargebee or Braintree, so a renewal, upgrade, downgrade or refund reprices the affiliate's payout without anyone touching a spreadsheet.
- Affiliate, referral and influencer programs run from one dashboard, which replaces duct-taping three tools for partner types that all send paying customers.
- Setup is self-serve, typically under 15 minutes, with no developer.
- Features: multi-tier and dual-sided rewards, custom rules per partner or plan, sub-affiliates up to three levels deep, link and coupon-code tracking, CPL/CPA, fraud protection, broadcast emails, contests and leaderboards, and automated payouts with tax forms and EU invoicing.
- Stated proof points: over a decade running subscription partner programs, $2B+ affiliate revenue tracked across 3,000+ SaaS customers, and JustCall driving $2.4M ARR through its affiliate channel.
- Pricing: Starter $49/month (up to $5,000/month affiliate revenue), Business $99/month (up to $15,000), Enterprise from $149/month above $15,000. 14-day free trial, no credit card.
- Downsides he names: a smaller G2 review base (30+) than PartnerStack (900+), custom pricing above roughly $15K/month, and less useful if you aren't subscription-based.

## Rewardful: best budget pick for Stripe or Paddle startups

- Dustin recommends Rewardful for early-stage SaaS that runs entirely on Stripe or Paddle and wants the simplest possible setup with no sales process.
- 0% transaction fees on every plan, unlimited affiliates and visitors on every tier, self-referral fraud detection, PayPal and Wise bulk payouts.
- Pricing: Starter $49/month (up to $7,500/month tracked revenue, 1 campaign, 2 team members), Growth $99/month (up to $15,000, unlimited campaigns, branded portal with custom domain), Enterprise $149+/month above $15,000. 14-day free trial, 30-day refund guarantee, month-to-month.
- Downsides: Stripe and Paddle only (a dealbreaker for Chargebee, Recurly or Braintree), no influencer-program management, and the custom domain is locked behind the $99 tier.

## Tolt and Tapfiliate: portal branding vs mixed business models

**Tolt** (best for a highly branded affiliate portal):
- Stripe, Paddle and Chargebee only (no Recurly or Braintree). 0% commission on payouts at every tier.
- Custom-domain branded portal on the entry tier, where competitors often gate it higher.
- Pricing: Basic $69/month (up to $10,000/month affiliate revenue, manual payouts, 2 programs), Growth $99/month (up to $20,000, auto payouts with a 2% processing fee, 5 programs), Pro $199/month (up to $50,000, unlimited programs, dedicated Slack), Enterprise custom above $50,000.
- Lower revenue ceilings mean an upgrade comes sooner. It is a newer, smaller player.

**Tapfiliate** (best for subscription brands that also sell one-time products):
- Fits a SaaS tool with an add-on marketplace or a subscription box that also sells gift cards.
- Supports recurring, one-time and multi-level commissions (multi-level on Scale and above), 30+ native integrations, and coupon-code tracking.
- Pricing: Launch $89/month ($74 annual; 1 program, 50 affiliates, 5,000 clicks/500 conversions), Scale $179/month ($149 annual; unlimited programs and affiliates, 100,000 clicks/10,000 conversions), Enterprise custom with full white-label.
- Downsides: overage fees on click and conversion caps, and it is less purpose-built for upgrade and downgrade events than FirstPromoter or Rewardful.

## PartnerStack, ClickGo and Everflow: when you need more than affiliate tracking

- **PartnerStack** suits B2B SaaS building a full partner ecosystem (affiliates, referral partners, reseller deal registration).
  - It has a partner marketplace that actively matches partners to programs, CRM integrations, and partner onboarding/certification tools.
  - Pricing is quote-based across Launch, Growth and Enterprise, and implementation takes weeks.
  - It is heavier than most subscription businesses need if they only run an affiliate program.
- **ClickGo** is built for networks, agencies and advertisers running programs at volume.
  - Strengths are proactive fraud interception before payout and full-funnel multi-source attribution.
  - Dustin notes its site mentions no subscription billing or Stripe/Paddle/Chargebee integration, so it earns its place for fraud protection, not billing sync.
  - Pricing is quote-based.
- **Everflow** is the enterprise option, unifying affiliate with paid (Google, Facebook, TikTok) and organic attribution in one layer.
  - It tracks recurring revenue and upsells but is not natively tied to subscription billing events.
  - Includes SOC 2 compliance, HubSpot/Salesforce integrations, a Customer Success Manager, and Everflow Pay.
  - Pricing is quote-based with a 6-month minimum contract.

## Dustin's decision rules

- Single best fit for a subscription business: FirstPromoter, as the only tool he lists built around billing-lifecycle commissions while unifying affiliate, referral and influencer programs, with fast self-serve setup and transparent pricing.
- Stripe- or Paddle-only and want the simplest, cheapest start: Rewardful.
- Building a full B2B partner ecosystem rather than a straightforward affiliate program: PartnerStack.
- Need affiliate unified with paid and organic attribution: Everflow, accepting a sales process and 6-month contract.
- Mixed subscription and one-time products, or multi-level commissions: Tapfiliate.
- Fraud detection and multi-network volume over billing sync: ClickGo.
