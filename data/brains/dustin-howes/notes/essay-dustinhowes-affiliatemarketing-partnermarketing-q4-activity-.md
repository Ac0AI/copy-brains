## Affiliate leaderboards and public gamification

- Dustin notes that some brands now publish scoreboards of which affiliates are winning.
- He is unsure whether that is a good idea. As a competitor, he likes seeing it.
- His theory is that affiliates want to be on the pedestal and get the extra attention.
- He sees gamification as great in some areas, but flags the risk when "you just see the same people win over and over again."
- He calls it a "mixed bag."

## Information sharing in affiliate has flipped

- Dustin recalls a conference moment from early in the industry where he asked a 10-plus-year agency owner or program manager for his top five publishers. The person gave a rude answer and walked away, saying it was inappropriate to ask.
- His view: people guarded that information 15 years ago because it was hard to find. Now "everybody yaps about everybody publicly."
- It is one of his biggest pet peeves when people keep that kind of information locked down.

## Short-runway webinar that outperformed

- Dustin ran a webinar with Alfie that walked through the whole platform. It was planned for 30 minutes and ran 60.
- Nearly 100 people commented, and the session was very interactive.
- He had roughly a one-week runway, effectively 3 to 4 days in a short week.
- The recording passed 1,000 views.
- Customers such as Wade Tonkin showed up, interacted and shared how much they like the tool.
- Result: the company asked him to do more webinars, so he is now building new ones and expediting the ones already in the pipeline.
