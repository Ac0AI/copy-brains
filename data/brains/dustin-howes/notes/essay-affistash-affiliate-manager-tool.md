## Affistash: what it is and who it is for

- Dustin describes Affistash as a SaaS platform launched in 2023 with the mission of being the most helpful tool available for affiliate managers.
- It is built for SMB brands that already have an affiliate program, which often lack resources and manpower, so time-saving features matter most to them.
- Dustin says he is "ALWAYS looking for the next game-changing tool." When he found Affistash he "recognized the power behind it" and wanted to be involved.
- Over about a year he helped shape it into what he calls "the ultimate affiliate management time saver," and he became its CMO after recognizing its potential.
- Core promise: generate a high-potential partner hitlist in minutes and save affiliate managers hours.

## Partner discovery features Dustin highlights

- **Whole-internet search.** Unlike competitor tools that rely on limited databases, Affistash scours the entire internet for potential affiliates and influencers.
- **Real-time ranking identification.** It finds partners currently ranking for relevant keywords, so you target affiliates already performing in your niche and more likely to produce results.
- **Lookalike discovery.** Give it the URL of an ideal partner and it finds hundreds of similar partners, which lets you replicate a successful partnership.
- **Competitor partner finder.** It surfaces websites that link to your competitors, so you can tap established affiliate relationships and try to sway them to your brand.
- **Traffic stats.** Every potential partner comes with detailed traffic data, so you can decide which affiliates to pursue based on data.
- The platform pulls from several APIs of top industry tools rather than relying only on its own algorithms, which gives users broader and more current data.

## Relationship management and outreach features

- **Centralized CRM portal** built specifically for partnership managers. It tracks existing and potential partners in one place and includes note-taking and relationship tracking.
- **AI-powered outreach.** It creates personalized outreach messages and timed email drip campaigns, which saves manual work while keeping a personal touch.
- **Custom AI assistant** that helps craft personalized messages that resonate with potential partners.
- **Integrations** with other marketing and analytics tools, so it slots into an existing stack with minimal disruption.
- **Support.** There is an Affistash Academy with educational resources on using the features, plus a dedicated customer support team.

He says Affistash fits an SMB brand with an affiliate program that wants to:

- Streamline partner discovery.
