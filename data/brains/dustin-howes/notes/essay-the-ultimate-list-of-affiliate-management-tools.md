## Affiliate recruitment is the most time-saving category to tool up

Dustin says recruitment is his favorite tooling category, partly because he's "a nerd with it" and partly because it saves the most time on the most tedious work. When he started managing programs, everything was manual: the most sophisticated method was searching Google for organic results and hoping to find an email address on a site. He has spent years giving product feedback to recruitment-tool companies including 5IQ, Grovia, Media Rails and Affistash, so he says he knows what affiliate managers need to be efficient.

His recruitment stack, by job:
- **Prospecting and outreach automation:** Alfie (finds affiliates, writes personalized outreach, automates follow-ups), Affistash (AI platform for finding affiliate and influencer partners plus contact info), AffiliateFinder (scans the web for creators already promoting competitors or relevant products).
- **Influencer discovery and data:** Upfluence, Influencers Club (data enrichment), Influencity (search by performance, niche, engagement), GRIN (discovery and recruitment, integrated with ecommerce tools).
- **Scraping:** Phantom Buster for scraping Instagram followers at scale, combined with Upfluence or Influencers Club.
- **Existing affiliate databases and lookalikes:** Publisher Discovery (huge database of existing affiliates), Similar Sites (find similar websites for partnership opportunities).
- **SEO and audience research:** Semrush, Respona (SEO player discovery with a built-in outreach drip system), SparkToro (where the target audience spends time online).
- **Outreach management:** Buzzstream (outreach and backlink campaigns), Affiliate Email Pro (AI tool for unique recruitment messages aimed at opens and responses).

## Network sourcing and LinkedIn prospecting

For finding people and offers inside the affiliate ecosystem, Dustin leans on three tools:
- **LinkedIn:** the "ultimate networking platform for business." He says affiliates are on there but "hidden in plain site"; use filters and search to prospect the right contact at companies.
- **Waalaxy:** LinkedIn automation for cold outreach to affiliate partners.
- **FMTC:** aggregates affiliate offers, deals and merchant data.

## Tracking, analytics and conversion optimization

Dustin groups tracking and analytics tools by what they do for a program:
- **Partnership platforms and tracking:** Impact.com (tracking, payouts, fraud detection, partnership management), Everflow (advanced partner tracking with granular analytics and optimization tools).
- **Dashboards and insight:** Vstat (visualizing program performance), Affluent (analytics for agencies and brands), Refinery (turns complex data into insights to drive revenue, acquire customers, optimize spend and reduce risk).
- **Verification and creators:** Get Affirmative (quickly check whether publisher sites have confirmed affiliate links), Favikon (tracks creator performance across social channels, AI categorization for finding influencers in any niche).
- **SaaS:** Rewardful, affiliate management software built for SaaS businesses.
- **Tracking accuracy:** Stape provides server-side tracking and Google Tag Manager hosting, capturing data that browsers and ad blockers would otherwise miss.

## Landing page conversion as part of affiliate tracking (Inflektion)

Dustin pairs affiliate tracking with conversion rate optimization through his own firm, Inflektion. His example is Oura Ring with Lindsey Vonn: a dedicated lander that speaks to her fans and to the benefits she finds valuable, with her recognizable face, her accolades and a quote. He says these elements together improved conversion rates by 200% over the basic home page. He describes this as what Inflektion does for all its clients so that affiliate and influencer programs "shine."

## Influencer and creator CRM

For managing relationships and pipelines, Dustin lists:
- **HubSpot:** powerful CRM with customizable pipelines for affiliate and influencer campaigns.
- **HighLevel:** all-in-one marketing platform with tracking, automation and CRM; he calls it great for agencies and consultants.
- **Copper:** CRM that integrates with Gmail and helps manage influencer outreach.
- **Aspire:** influencer marketing platform with CRM features and campaign workflows.
- **CreatorIQ:** enterprise-level influencer CRM with integrations and performance tracking.
- **Modash:** influencer discovery and CRM for UGC and affiliate programs.
- **Influencer.co:** influencer relationship platform for managing campaigns.

## Automation, AI and virtual assistants for the manager's own workflow

Dustin's time-saving stack for the affiliate manager:
- **Scraping and email finding:** Instant Data Scraper (browser extension for tabular data), Hunter.io (professional email addresses), Browse AI (no-code extraction of affiliate info from sites and marketplaces).
- **Workflow:** Make (Integromat) to connect tools and data, TextExpander for text shortcuts on repetitive emails and outreach, Notion (notes, tasks, CRM, wikis), SaneBox (declutters inboxes).
- **Dictation:** Typeless converts speech to text and corrects grammar.
- **Link checking:** Redirect Path, a Chrome plugin that finds broken or redirected links affecting affiliate tracking.
- **Delegation:** OnlineJobs.ph to hire affordable virtual assistants for program management tasks.
- **Access:** NordPass password manager to streamline logins across affiliate platforms.
- **AI writing and analysis:** ChatGPT (email templates, outreach copy, reports), Jasper (content, emails, blogs at scale), Grammarly (polishing), Relevance AI (clustering influencer content and analyzing creator fit), Prezi (AI presentations).

## Fraud, compliance, payments and engagement

Dustin treats compliance and payouts as their own layer of the stack:
- **Brand and traffic compliance:** Integrishield (monitors campaigns for brand compliance), Rightlander (finds affiliates' and sub-networks' active traffic sources and monitors sites and social content for non-compliant marketing), BrandVerity (monitors paid search and coupon use), Revvim (stops wasted SEM spend, also good for trademark-plus protection).
- **Fraud detection:** FraudBlock (coupon and attribution fraud), Anura (ensures clicks and conversions are human), Forensiq (affiliate fraud across channels), Voluum (performance tracking for affiliates and media buyers).
- **Payments:** Tipalti (global mass payments with compliance and tax features), PayPal Payouts (bulk payments to affiliates worldwide).
- **Communication and engagement:** Slack (affiliate communities), ActiveCampaign (email automation and CRM), EZ Messenger (Facebook Messenger automation), TubeBuddy (YouTube channel management), SocialBlade (track influencer performance), Keywords Everywhere (keyword research).

## How he frames choosing tools

Dustin says program effectiveness "hinges on selecting the right tools tailored to your goals and program scale." Whether recruiting high performers, optimizing UGC campaigns or tracking complex multi-touch attribution, he argues the tech stack "can make or break your efforts." He positions the right tools as saving time and effort while adding insight and automation that can streamline processes, optimize budget and lift a program's results.
