## The constellation strategy: aligning influencer and affiliate along the purchase path

Dustin hosts this panel, and his own framing is that influencer buzz alone isn't enough and affiliate conversions are increasingly scrutinized. He presents the "constellation strategy" (a concept from panelist Kim Murray) as a way to align influencer content distribution with affiliate retargeting and close the loop with data-based performance.

- Brands need measurable action, a scalable approach to growth, and a way to move customers down the funnel predictably.
- He wants to track incrementality and ROAS at the publisher level.
- He frames the aim as helping brands scale affiliate and influencer programs creatively, especially in the early stages of growth.

## Incrementality as the real question in partnerships

Dustin introduces incrementality as a buzzword the panel unpacks, asking why it matters "more now than ever." He pushes the point that the lack of standardized data hurts publishers too.

- He notes there is a huge gap in a standardized way of delivering data to publishers so they can optimize.
- If publishers provide brand growth and recognition but can't see the data, it is harder to optimize toward that and quantify it.
- He points out that affiliate tracking solutions are "not all built the same": some give far more dynamic visibility into what happens in the funnel, and asks what gaps most networks are missing.

## Where the funnel measurement breaks

Dustin probes where attribution fails and where each partner type sits in the customer journey.

- He asks how a major media publisher (USA Today) views its role from awareness to conversion.
- He asks how influencer content can "actually convert or play a role in driving performance," since many brands treat influencer as purely top-of-funnel.
- He asks what top-of-funnel strategies help close on the funnel, and what tools give merchants insight into traction through each stage.
- He observes that the data partner-level tools collect also lets agencies tailor campaigns for new brands to the part of the funnel where they find value.

## Cookie windows, budget caps and "too successful" campaigns

Dustin raises tough practical objections and relays audience questions.

- On cookie windows, he relays a viewer question that extending tracking beyond the standard 30-day window would help surface publishers driving high-quality upper-funnel traffic. He adds the brand-side counterpoint: a longer duration means paying partners more, since internal email and retargeting campaigns still have to pay out the affiliate.
- He asks whether a publisher would avoid a brand with a 10-day cookie versus one with 90 days.
- On budget caps, he calls it "ludicrous talk" when brands say a campaign is too successful and want to stop it, because "it's on performance." He asks how to respond when brands say they need to halt campaigns.
- He asks about vanity or closing coupons: whether publishers request a closing coupon when creating content with brands.

## Content, boosting and CPC

Dustin steers the discussion to tactics that are hot in the space.

- He asks whether boosting influencer posts that make great content has good value.
- He highlights CPC as "super hot" right now, with more and more affiliates adopting the model and finding value, and asks how premium editorial placements are evolving as brands want more measurable ROI, ROAS and incrementality.
- He names Linkby, Beehiiv and Goloot (as he describes them, "super hot right now") among the tools in that space.

## Practical close: getting started and unifying programs

For brands under eight figures, Dustin asks for low-lift, high-impact tactics to scale affiliate and influencer programs. He closes by asking what to do first tomorrow if influencer and affiliate are run separately.

- He asks Kim about the tactic specifically for sub-eight-figure brands.
- He asks the panel for the single first step to unify a split affiliate/influencer approach.
- He sponsors the session with Inflection, a tracking solution he describes as evolving affiliate tracking toward personalization and conversion optimization. He cites brands like Ring, Backbone and Power Plate, and says a brand manager can build a fully customized landing page "in less than 10 minutes" that tells the influencer's story to lift conversion rate.
