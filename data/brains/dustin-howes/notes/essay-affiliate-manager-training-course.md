## Who the course is for and what it promises

- Dustin sells a 10-weekday affiliate manager training ("Performance Manager Training", $697 one-time) built from 12+ years of experience compressed into 200+ PowerPoint slides.
- Target buyers: business owners training themselves or their team, agencies wanting a certified performance/affiliate manager credential, students, and marketing employees.
- Time commitment: one hour per day for 10 consecutive weekdays. Optional 30-minute assignments follow each session, though he strongly suggests implementing lessons while they're fresh.
- His framing: "You don't know…what you don't know." The biggest pitfall for an affiliate manager is not knowing where to focus, because the job is never done and there are always more partners to recruit.

## What an affiliate manager actually is

- He defines an affiliate manager as a partnership development representative of the company.
- The focus is growing the affiliate program, but there are dozens of small daily tasks needed to maximize those efforts.
- His position is that a good affiliate program will represent about 20% of the revenue from marketing efforts and will likely have the lowest CPA of all marketing channels, besides organic.

## Curriculum: the order for building a program

The course walks through the program lifecycle in this sequence:
1. Affiliate 101
2. Product research
3. Competitive analysis
4. Choosing the right platform
5. Creating the program
6. Publisher types
7. Recruitment strategy
8. Negotiations
9. Activation
10. Building partnerships
11. Fraud prevention and auditing
12. Creative campaigns
13. Program maintenance
14. Reporting
15. "The Little Things"

The implied approach: research the product and competitors and pick the platform before creating the program, then recruit, negotiate, activate, protect, and maintain.
