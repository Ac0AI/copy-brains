## Media Kids: building and selling a creator economy company

- Casey co-founded Media Kids (mediakids.com) with his co-founder Kieran. It is creator economy software that lets creators, podcasters and musicians aggregate all of their social media data in one place.
- The company raised money in 2020 and was acquired in September 2023.
- His fiancée Jacqlyn was COO and an integral part of the company's growth.
- His new company is Listener FM, named after his "Listener" Instagram handle.
- Casey was 23 at the time of this recording. He started his podcast at 17 and has done it for 6 years.

## Interviewing Larry King: how a relationship, not an ask, landed the interview

Casey tells this as his most impactful interview story. The steps:

1. In 2019 a friend, Michael Grew, was helping Tilman Fertitta promote his book "Shut Up and Listen". Tilman was the first billionaire Casey interviewed, over Zoom.
2. A couple of weeks later Tilman's team invited about 25 people who helped promote the book to Mastro's in Beverly Hills.
3. Kieran ended up sitting next to Chance King, Larry King's son. Tilman said at the table that Larry had been an inspiration to him all his life.
4. Casey and Chance hit it off and became friends. Casey invited him to an event he was hosting in Los Angeles the following weekend.
5. Casey never asked for his dad on the show. He says he is "not one that would ever ask, hey, can I have your dad on my show". The timing and the organic relationship did the work.
6. On Thanksgiving 2019 he texted Chance. Chance said Larry had recently had a stroke but would love to be on the show when he felt better.
7. The following week Casey and Jacqlyn ran into Larry King in his wheelchair at the Century City mall. He sent Chance a photo.
8. Two weeks later Casey was interviewing Larry in his studio. Casey was 19 and Larry was 87, with about 60,000 interviews and the last 10 presidents behind him. This was 6 to 8 months before Larry passed.

Casey has done nearly 500 interviews. He says this one left the deepest imprint on his interview approach.

## Listening as a business and relationship skill

- Larry King told him on the show: "You'll never learn anything when you're talking." Casey took this on as a core principle and built his brand identity around it (the Listener handle, Listener FM).
- He argues that anyone who wants to be great in business or in relationships needs a heavy emphasis on listening.
- The failure mode he describes is a dinner where you share your perspective the whole time and never ask the other person about their day.
- In his relationship, he says listening to the other person's perspective during fights and differences matters most after commitment, because that is how you grow with someone.

## Vetting people and networking

- Don't judge someone you don't know. A clip on social media or one rude moment in a hallway should not define them. He notes the persona someone has online is not always the person.
- Time is how you get to know someone, so spend quality time with people before deciding whether they belong in your life.
- Having interviewed people from Larry King to Rick Ross to Elon Musk's mom and sister, he says everyone is human, dealing with their own problems and trying to get better.
- In a transactional world, his first question on meeting someone is "how can I bring value to this person's life". That might be a quick introduction or being thoughtful about something he has dealt with himself.

## Commitment and focus

- He compares dating abundance (apps, Instagram DMs) to business: in a world of many options you need to commit to something. "You can't be the person just trying to do everything because you're going to go nowhere."
- He draws a contrast with business. If a company goes bankrupt you can move on to the next one, but a relationship has to be nurtured and "you can't just let it sink".
- He ties his happiness to a performance mindset from sports (hockey, lacrosse, football). He is most happy when he is getting better and progressing toward a goal, in sports or in business.

## Mentors and learning

- At 15 a football injury left him in a neck brace for 6 months and almost paralyzed. He was depressed and unsure what to do with his life. That is when he first picked up personal development books such as "Think and Grow Rich" and "How to Win Friends and Influence People". Those authors were his first mentors.
- His view of mentorship has shifted from "how do I ask someone to be my mentor" to learning from people around him.
- Aristotle Loumis was monumental to Media Kids' success and acts like a big brother on business advice. He will spend 30 minutes rewriting an email in a way Casey would never have thought to write.
- He finds mentors for specific areas. Guests taught him running technique and fueling before his first marathon last year.
- On the podcast he tries to take one lesson from every conversation and apply it right away.
