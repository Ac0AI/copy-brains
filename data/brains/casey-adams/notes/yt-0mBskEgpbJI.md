## Starting a podcast: Casey's three-step blueprint (via Chase's questions)

Casey's host-side material here is thin, since Chase (the guest) does most of the talking. The source is Chase Chewning's interview on Casey's show, and the substance (health coaching, military, monetization, daily routine) belongs to Chase, not Casey. The only content attributable to Casey is a few remarks about his own podcast setup and tactics.

## Start imperfect and upgrade quality later

- Casey began his podcast on a roughly $100 Blue Yeti microphone, and the first episode was recorded on AirPods, which he calls "terrible because of bluetooth."
- He says he "didn't care about the quality" at the start and was "so obsessed with putting it out there getting something going and going with that momentum."
- His principle: quality can always get better, but you can't wait for it to be perfect. Take imperfect action.
- He has since upgraded his full podcast setup (Shure microphones, new equipment sourced from Chase).

## Share to Spotify via Instagram Stories to drive downloads

- Tactic: tag the guest in an Instagram Story that carries the Spotify podcast link. If the guest reshares the story, they automatically reshare the Spotify link too, with no copy-paste or swipe-up needed.
- Casey says Rick Ross did this when he was a guest (11 million Instagram followers) and his downloads "went through the roof."
- Casey calls this the reason he'd recommend sharing via Spotify's native story share, because it removes friction for the guest.

## Use Zoom interviews to increase output and downloads

- During the pandemic Casey did 60 to 90 Zoom interviews, something he wouldn't have done otherwise.
- The high frequency of episodes, made easy by remote interviews, produced a large spike in downloads.
- In 2019 he had been trying to do mostly in-person interviews because he wanted to meet guests; remote recording made frequent publishing much easier.

## Why Casey does a podcast

- He tells people that starting a podcast is very beneficial to your brand and networking, and that is the main reason he does it.
- He says the payoff "comes back tenfold."
- His distinction: that brand and networking value is separate from directly monetizing the show.

## Casey's podcast listening habits

- He leans toward business, financial and news shows rather than interview shows, and names Robinhood Snacks as one of his favorites.
- He uses Spotify and notes that he often tags guests in his own Spotify shares.
