## Omni-channel campaign reporting on Listener.com

- Casey promotes Listener.com's omni-channel campaign reporting for sponsor campaigns that run across audio, YouTube and social.
- The pitch: every podcast, video and clip is reported in one place, instead of stitched together from separate platforms.
- Casey frames the product's purpose as giving a sales team "everything your team needs to sell, report, and renew with confidence." Reporting is positioned as a sales and renewal tool, not just a measurement feature.
