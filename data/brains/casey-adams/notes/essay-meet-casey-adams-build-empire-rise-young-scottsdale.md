## Origin story: injury to personal brand

- At 15, Casey was diagnosed with interspinous ligament damage while playing football.
- He spent six months in a neck brace, unable to move around much. That is when he got introduced to personal development.
- After months of reading books and building a positive attitude, he learned about the power of social media and decided to build a personal brand.
- By 2019 (three years later), he says it "truly changed my life forever." He has since traveled the world speaking on stages about personal branding.
- His core takeaway: people have a lot of power in how they position themselves online.

## Rise of The Young podcast as a distribution engine

- Casey started the podcast "Rise of The Young" at the end of 2017.
- It became a top 200 business podcast in the world, and he says his content and podcasts have reached millions of people.
- He used the show to sit down with some of the world's most successful entrepreneurs and leaders, interviewing them from age 18.
- He says interviewing impactful people that young gave him insight into "the power of listening."
- He positions himself as someone who specializes in interviewing world-class entrepreneurs, networking and connecting people, and building personal brands.
- His differentiator: in about three years, at only 18, he connected, networked, interviewed and collaborated with many influential entrepreneurs and business leaders.

## Build Your Empire and the events business

- Build Your Empire is his company, focused on showcasing world-class entrepreneurs through content and events.
- It is known for world-class business events, and its stated aim is revolutionizing the business event industry.
- He found a passion for hosting events and has thrown dozens of business events across the world.
- He co-hosted an event with Chris "Drama" Pfaff, founder and CEO of "Young & Reckless."
- His biggest lesson from the events: "there is power in bringing people together."

## Partnership with MOTI Chat

- Casey partnered with the app MOTI Chat for over a year as of 2019.
- He describes it as the #1 consulting app in the world: a peer-to-peer consulting app that changes how consultants and business professionals get paid for their time.
- He credits the experience of building with the MOTI team as a pleasure.

## Building a relationship-driven career

- Over three years he says he learned a great deal about communication skills and what it takes to build real, genuine relationships.
- Listening, gained through interviewing, is the skill he highlights most.
- His network, podcast guests and event partners all stem from the same habit: connecting and collaborating with influential people early.

## Running a company while in high school

- Time management was a major lesson. He ran a company while going to school and learned where his time was best spent.
- Example: he had his phone taken away in math class, and the teacher had no idea he was texting a client about their Facebook ad campaign.
- He learned to say NO to opportunities. As an entrepreneur you are introduced to many opportunities, and learning to say no "has truly impacted my life."
- He also learned when to distance himself from people: "You must know when to let go, and when to move on from a situation or an individual."

## Mindset: luck and support systems

- He defines "luck" partly as being born when he was: a thriving economy where technology is accessible to anybody.
- He calls access to today's technology "such a blessing."
- He counts being born into a caring family as true luck.
- He calls having a support system of people who truly care about you "the most important asset I have."
