## Podcast reporting undersells what shows deliver

- Casey argues the podcast industry is underselling itself. A show today is audio, YouTube, and social content, and innovative brands are paying for all of it.
- Modern campaigns succeed when they are sold and measured across those channels, not audio alone.
- Most campaign reports still lead with downloads, which he calls "only one piece of what a show or network delivers."
- His diagnosis: reporting that stops at downloads understates the value of a show or network and weakens the case to brand partners.

## Positioning Listener.com around omni-channel campaign reporting

- Heading into Q4, Casey's team sharpened Listener.com around one thing: omni-channel campaign reporting for the modern podcast industry.
- The product is positioned as a fix for a specific, felt problem (reporting that undersells shows) rather than as a broad platform.
- The call to action is aimed at that pain: "If your reporting is underselling your shows, let's fix that." Interested networks book a call with the team.

## The Listener workflow for networks and shows

Casey lays out the workflow as four steps:

1. Pitch your network with one link.
2. Prove your true reach across episodes.
3. Run campaigns and watch pacing live.
4. Report results your brand partners can see for themselves.

- The sequence covers the full sales cycle: pitching, proving reach, delivering and monitoring, then reporting.
- The last step is built on transparency: brand partners see the results themselves instead of relying on a network's summary.

## How to sell podcast advertising more effectively

- Sell the whole show: bundle audio, YouTube, and social content into the pitch, since that is what brands are already buying.
- Prove reach across episodes, not just single-episode downloads.
- Monitor campaign pacing live so delivery issues surface during the campaign instead of in the post-mortem.
- Give brand partners direct visibility into results so the report stands on its own.
