## Hiring: the VC training program (April 2026)

- Jason offers four slots in a "world's best VC training program" for recent graduates or career changers. Tuition is $0, and pay is "what you would have spent on your MBA tuition."
- Workload: about 60 hours a week, or 10-12 hours a day, plus checking email on weekends.
- Path: researcher, then analyst, then associate. He says "1.5 in three" make it to associate.
- The four traits he screens for:
  1. Being a learning machine
  2. High executive function
  3. Being extremely focused and curious
  4. Being able to work 12 hours a day for the founders
- Selection: he accepts under 1% of applicants and doesn't care how fancy the degree is. He loves candidates from UT Austin and "folks with a chip on their shoulders."
- Application: a cover letter on why you want to be a venture capitalist, your current skills and examples of your work ethic, sent to researchers@launch.co.
- His edge thesis: "80% of VCs are lazy AF," so simply showing up and working 10-12 hours a day beats most of them.

## LAUNCH Festival 2026 (San Francisco, March 16-17)

- Origin: Jason created TechCrunch50 / LAUNCH Festival about 20 years ago because networking events cost about $4,000 a ticket and demoing on stage cost about $20,000. He ran it for about 10 years, paused after COVID, and brought it back in 2026.
- Format:
  - 50 startups each demo for three minutes
  - Judges give feedback and pick favorites
  - 250 founders attend free
  - 100 investors and LPs with whom he co-invests attend as guests
  - 50 VIP seats for the public at $1,000 each, which "unwrite" the free founder tickets
- Theme: OpenClaw, AI and agents.
- Prizes: Jason invests $25,000 in the best OpenClaw startup and $25,000 in the best non-OpenClaw startup, on the terms of the winner's last round or a Note/SAFE for the next round.
- Lesson learned: previously, "some bad actors" claimed a $100M valuation to game the prize. He now asks winners to act in good faith because he wants to invest in real startups.
- He also invites others to match his investment through partners@launch.co and sponsors to buy breakfast or the post-event mixer. The top ten startups from his Founder University Tokyo program are included too.
- His stated motive: "to make the industry open to more people on the way up."
