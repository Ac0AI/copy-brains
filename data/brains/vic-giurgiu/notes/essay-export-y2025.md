## Business trajectory and timeline in 2025

- Vic started 2025 after what he calls "the success of 2024", with Affistash at $4,000 MRR in January.
- Affistash reached $6,000 MRR by March.
- In March he also signed a $1,300 MRR customer for a lead generation subscription tool he built in stealth. He tweeted about it, which sparked the discussion that led to his first acquisition of the year.
- He launched PodKit (a media kit tool for podcasters) in February and had revenue the same week.
- In April he sold PodKit to Listener.com and joined as founding member and Head of Engineering (role started in July).
- In late November he sold Affistash to FMTC. He calls it life-changing, though "far from an amount that would set me up for retirement."
- By the end of the year he describes his investment portfolio at a 55% rate of return.

## How PodKit started: partnering through a warm intro

- Vic's Affistash co-founder Dustin introduced him to Eric, who owns a B2B podcast network (B2B Pod Pros) and works with podcasters.
- The pitch was simply "why don't you work together on PodKit?" Vic said yes.
- Eric became co-founder on PodKit.
- The tool launched in February and had revenue within the same week.
- Takeaway Vic implies: a partner with direct access to the target customer (podcasters) shortens the path to first revenue.

## Selling PodKit and joining the acquirer

- Casey Adams of Listener.com messaged Vic about potentially acquiring PodKit. Vic was at a party when the message came in.
- He sold PodKit in April, only about two months after launch, and joined Listener's team as founding member and Head of Engineering.
- He values the learning from working alongside people he admires (Casey Adams, Kieran O'Brien).
- Work at Listener included a Megaphone integration (built partly while on a family vacation in Greece) and a big product launch in December.
- Listener is changing its market positioning in 2026 after that launch, which he sees as large growth potential.

## Exiting Affistash

- In August, while on vacation, Vic started thinking about exiting Affistash, his most profitable business to date.
- Affistash was co-founded with Dustin.
- He sold it to FMTC at the end of November.
- Effect on him: more confidence in his "building capabilities" and readiness to "do things bigger and better than ever."
- He celebrated by taking his best friends to dinner.

## Community and surrounding yourself with builders

- A Twitter DM from Andrei (Bantu) in February led him to ClujHouse, a place for young people in Romania to build together. Before it, he felt "alone" in his interest in startups, with only David and Alex to talk shop with.
- He describes ClujHouse as a "brotherhood" or "fellowship" he had been seeking for a while.
- The space moved from a corporate office to a mansion in downtown Cluj and joined the LocalhostHQ network, which brought introductions to people like Kei and Hardeep.
- Months later, he and his ClujHouse friends were each in better positions with their businesses than ever.

## Mindset: money, mortality and the drive to create

- A shoulder skin surgery and a two-week cancer scare in November (it turned out to be a skin infection) changed how he views life.
- He says he does not want the mentality he had at 16-17, "chasing money and thinking that would somehow make everyone love me."
- On ambition: "the higher I get, the more I want to climb," and he accepts and embraces that.
- He concludes that "the purpose of my life is to create": whether an idea succeeds or fails, he feels the need to bring it into the world, the same feeling since his first YouTube video at age 11.
- He treats the work-life balance practically: he kept working 6-8 hours a day while travelling, and notes the exhaustion of pairing that with heavy nightlife.
- Using money well: he did charity with friends at Christmas and calls it "genuinely one of the best things money can do."

## 2025 outputs and plans for 2026

- He launched GameCap, his first open source project, and wants to do more open source work.
- He started apartment shopping to buy his first property and move out early in 2026, funded by the year's business growth.
- He is studying AI at UBB Cluj but does the bare minimum, because the degree competes with focusing on the work where he makes his money.
- For 2026 he is "more hungry than ever to build something big" and aims to grow his role at Listener.
