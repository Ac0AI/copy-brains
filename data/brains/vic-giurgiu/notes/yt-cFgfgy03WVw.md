## How Affistash works: AI matching from company data to partner leads

Vic built Affistash (the product is also called "FS" / Affy stash in the transcript) in early 2023 as an AI tool for affiliate managers. He started by chatting with a few affiliate managers to find "the best idea and the best tool that can be built specifically for affiliate managers using the power of AI." The tool recruits affiliates, publishers and partners for one or more affiliate programs.

- The user inputs company data once: name, niche, website, business model, geolocation, price range, subscription model (one time, recurring, per usage) and a short description.
- The algorithm uses this to understand what the company does and who its ideal affiliates are.
- Vic's demo company was "Sofa Brain", an AI SaaS for interior designers and real estate agents.
- A business can run recruitment for multiple affiliate programs from one dashboard.

## Discovery: partner types, match rate and the feedback loop

- Partner types searchable: affiliate partners (a mix of everything), content creators, blog/media publishers (SEO), agencies and newsletters. Each type supports a different set of platforms.
- Each lead shows a sample post, a partner type classification and a match rate estimated by the AI. A higher match rate means a better fit.
- The demo surfaced "top 10 AI interior design tools" listicles and "AI for real estate agents" articles, publishers already ranking in search and monetizing with AdSense and affiliate links.
- Each lead has a "get contacts" button returning email plus LinkedIn (and Twitter), so outreach can happen on more than one channel.
- Up/down votes train the algorithm. Upvoting a publisher returns more leads like it (e.g. housing.com); downvoting teaches it to avoid that type of partner.
- Refreshing opens a feedback pop-up (what you liked and didn't like), and a new lead list arrives in about two to three minutes. His demo refresh returned 112 leads.
- Leads can be exported as CSV or shared with teammates by email.
- The more the platform is used, the better the results get.

## Outreach: AI email templates and recruitment campaigns

- Users connect their mailbox and run recruitment campaigns inside the tool. It tracks emails sent and emails that failed to deliver.
- An AI template library composes outreach using the company data already entered and the details known about the publisher. Options include style (e.g. casual) and length (e.g. under 100 words).
- Vic recommends personalizing each message for the specific partner: it "obviously results in way better open rates, way better conversion rates." The AI template is meant to get you the first draft so you "move way faster."
- Replies land in the user's own mailbox, so conversations continue there.

## Manual partner search engine

- Besides automated matching, Affistash has a manual search where the user enters their own short or long keywords, similar to tools like Gro/"roia"-style SEO partner finders (the name is unclear in the transcript).
- It also searches social and B2B platforms such as LinkedIn and Telegram (to find communities), not only SEO results.
- Vic positions it as a way to get even more leads than the automated engine finds.

## Pricing and packaging

- Individual affiliate managers: 450 leads per month for $47/month on the monthly plan, or $37/month on the annual plan.
- Enterprise plan for teams: more than 1,000 leads per month and 6 dashboard seats, at $257/month, or $128/month on the annual plan.
- Vic's argument: price per lead is low because the discovery and outreach are automated.
- Product design choices: contextual help text on features, a live demo in the dashboard for teammates, and "talk to an expert" buttons at setup steps so the team can assist customers.

## Roadmap

- Next planned feature: AI-assisted onboarding of new partners and handling onboarding conversations, once the AI is "well enough trained."
- Stated goal: automate as much of the recruitment process as possible, from discovering new partner leads to outreaching them and closing partnerships "as fast as possible."
