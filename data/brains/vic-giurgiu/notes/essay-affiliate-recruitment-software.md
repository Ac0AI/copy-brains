## Affistash: what it is and who it's for

- Vic built Affistash (also written "Afstache" in the episode notes) as affiliate recruitment software that automates lead discovery for affiliate and partner managers.
- Target user: affiliate and partner managers, particularly in the SaaS industry.
- He started working at an affiliate marketing agency at 15 and was 17 when he built and launched Affistash while still in high school.
- The name is tied to the affiliate industry itself.
- Core promise: take the manual work out of finding partners. Instead of hunting for affiliates by hand, a manager gets a list of relevant, contactable partners.

## How the product works

- The dashboard shows stats, educational demos and a list of discovered leads.
- AI-driven partner discovery generates leads for different partner types, from content creators to media publishers.
- Each lead comes with contact details such as email and LinkedIn.
- Users connect their own mailbox and send outreach from inside the tool, using AI-generated email templates tailored to the partner type.
- Data sources are a mix of a manually collected database and APIs/scrapers for platforms like YouTube, LinkedIn and Twitter.
- Vic offers personalized demos to people who reach out.

## Validating the idea before building

- Vic's research approach was to validate Affistash as an affiliate recruitment tool through community engagement, talking to the people who would use it rather than assuming demand.
- He stresses idea validation as the first step, ahead of building.
- He credits networking and mentorship as major factors in getting a product to market as a teenager.
- He advises picking a niche you are passionate about, since the work of refining the product is long and sustained.

## Pricing

- Plans are built to be affordable and flexible, from individual marketers up to enterprise teams.
- The enterprise plan is aimed at teams running multiple affiliate programs.
- The interviewer's reaction was that the pricing is low and the tool may be underpriced for the value it delivers. That is the host's view, not Vic's.

## Where Vic sees affiliate marketing going

- He expects more automation and AI across the affiliate workflow.
- The areas he expects to be optimized first are recruitment, onboarding and commission processing.
- Affistash's role is to move recruitment from manual prospecting toward automated, AI-assisted discovery and outreach.

## Work ethic and shipping habits

- Vic worked late at night to redesign the Affistash landing page, and defends that as part of continually refining and improving the product for affiliate managers.
- He balances running the business with school, treating the product as something that needs ongoing iteration rather than a one-time launch.

## Distribution tactics for outreach

- The interview's advice to managers is to connect with prospects on LinkedIn alongside email, and to use AI-generated templates to speed up outreach. Vic's product is built around that combined contact-plus-template workflow.
- He offers personalized demos and invites direct engagement on LinkedIn and Twitter as a way to reach his own buyers.
