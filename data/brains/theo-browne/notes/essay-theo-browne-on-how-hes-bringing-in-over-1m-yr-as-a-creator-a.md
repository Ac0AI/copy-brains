## Who Theo is and what he works on

- Theo describes himself as a nerd, a software developer for nearly two decades, a popular tech YouTuber, CEO of T3 Tools, and an investor. He says he works on many projects.
- He currently focuses on his YouTube channel and T3 Tools' two main products, T3 Chat and T3 Code.
- Older products (Ping.gg, UploadThing, PicThing) are still maintained but get less focus "since they already serve our needs."

## Income split: creator vs founder

- Creator income is roughly $276,000 a year.
- As a founder, T3 Chat alone brings in seven-digit ARR, and he runs several other products alongside it.
- The article's header lists revenue of more than $100K a month across his work.
- T3 Chat reached seven-digit ARR "last year." None of his other products approach T3 Chat's revenue, though a few have similar reach.

## Portfolio approach: one product leads, others are kept alive

- Theo keeps several products running but concentrates attention on the one with the most momentum (T3 Chat) and a newer one (T3 Code).
- Products that already do their job for his own needs are maintained rather than expanded.
- Revenue is very uneven across the portfolio: one product carries most of the income, the rest are supporting products or reach plays.

## T3 Code: open source, unmonetized, used for reach

- T3 Code has over 60,000 users in just a few months.
- It is open source and not monetized.
- The example shows he is willing to ship a widely used product with no revenue attached, while the monetized product (T3 Chat) funds the business.
