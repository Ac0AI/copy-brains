## Reddit as a first-100-customers channel: scale, results and why it works

Romàn co-founded Gojiberry AI, an intent-based LinkedIn outreach SaaS. It was at $0 revenue about four months before the interview and was near $30K MRR (a dashboard showed about $24K MRR) by the time he spoke. Most users are on the $99 plan, which gives unlimited high-intent leads.

- Reddit was a main early acquisition channel. It produced "over 11 million" impressions and more than 40,000 website visitors.
- He says buying that reach would have cost "hundreds of thousands of dollars". It got the first roughly 100 customers.
- A hidden benefit: nearly every lead on a demo call or on another platform has already seen him on Reddit ("Oh, you are the Reddit guy"). He argues the more often a lead sees you, the more likely you close them on a call.
- His earlier SaaS, Coco AI, was scaled to $50K MRR with outreach and sold to a US venture. He credits the acquisition method for its success and built Gojiberry on the same logic.

## Post formula: story plus proof, with the product never pitched directly

Romàn's best posts are stories with evidence, not ads.

- **"I paid 5 influencers on LinkedIn to promote my SaaS. Here's what $1,250 got me."** It got 160K views and 543 upvotes, about 2,000 site visitors, and roughly 10 to 15 clients. He included the influencer posts and all available proof.
- **A Y Combinator rejection post** (selected for the interview, failed it). It got more than 179,000 views and roughly 15 clients.
- Rules he follows:
  - Tell a story and show proof so readers know it is real.
  - Never put the SaaS directly in the post. Trigger curiosity so people look it up.
  - Any event in your life can become a post, including a failure.
- If you have no success to share yet, write about how the SaaS is going and what problem you are trying to solve. He says anyone can get views and traffic this way.
- Ideas come from his daily work as a SaaS marketer: the more he does, the more stories he has.

## Writing workflow with AI

- Romàn is not a native English speaker. He tells the story to ChatGPT by voice, then has it translate and correct the text.
- He says speaking lets him go into far more detail than typing everything out.
- He uses ChatGPT plus Nano Banana for every post and graphic. This costs about $40/month.

## Account setup and warm-up playbook

1. Use a fresh account per browser. He runs one each on Chrome, Firefox and Safari, which he calls "more than enough to keep it safe".
2. Do not create the Reddit account with a brand-new email. He says new email plus new Reddit account gets you banned right away.
3. Add a profile picture, link the SaaS in the bio, and turn on the setting that hides your feed. Otherwise people who open your profile see you always talking about the same SaaS and conclude you advertise too much.
4. For the first 7 to 14 days do no marketing. Only comment and upvote to warm up the account and earn first karma. More karma means a stronger account.
5. After 7 to 14 days, start posting, then start marketing.
6. Skip subreddits that ban promotion or are moderated so tightly that posting is almost impossible.
7. Change the angle on every post. You can redirect to a YouTube video or a Twitter post, which he calls "the magic of Reddit".

## Getting a post seen: early upvotes and engagement

- Target at least 10 upvotes in the first 10 minutes (elsewhere he says as many as possible in the first 5 to 10 minutes).
- He has a group of friends who share each other's posts and upvote and comment on each one. Internally this is a group of 15 marketers. Early momentum then pushes the post to external users and ranks it higher.
- Reply to every single comment. This shows engagement, which brings more upvotes and a higher rank.

## Handling negativity and bans

- Show proof so no one can argue with you. For example, if you make $25K MRR, show it.
- Block people who insult you or are purely negative.
- He says it can be frightening at first, since a post can draw 15 bad comments saying you will never make it. You have to stick with it and believe in what you are doing.

## Gojiberry's marketing stack and costs

- Instantly AI for cold email: about $800/month, because of very high volume.
- Outrank for SEO: about $99/month.
- Gojiberry AI itself for LinkedIn and high-intent outreach: about $99/month.
- Cal.com-style booking tool for calls: about $20/month.
- Framer for the website and landing pages: about $49/month.
- ChatGPT plus Nano Banana for post and graphic creation: about $40/month.
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator on some accounts: about $100/month.

## Product and positioning

- Gojiberry finds high-intent leads on LinkedIn from signals: someone liking a specific post, engaging with a competitor, or having just raised funds or hired.
- Each day it surfaces the warmest leads, and users launch campaigns from the same tool. It automates connection requests and AI-personalized messages.
- Romàn says acceptance and reply rates are well above industry norms because the leads are strong and the messaging is tailored per lead.

## Core advice

- "Advertise the way you would love to be advertised to." He likes founder stories like Starter Story's, because hearing how someone succeeded makes him want to learn more, and he markets the same way.
