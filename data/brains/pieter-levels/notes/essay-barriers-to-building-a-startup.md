## The 2018 cost floor for starting a startup

Pieter argues that barriers to starting a startup had "never been so low" as of July 2018. He backs this with four concrete cost points:

- Laptop: a Chromebook costs $149.
- Learning: YouTube has free code tutorials.
- Domain: a domain name costs $0.99/year.
- Hosting: a VPS server costs $2.49/month.

His point is that money and access are no longer valid excuses. The total outlay to get a first product online is small enough that cost is not the real obstacle.

## The "what are you waiting for?" mindset

Pieter closes the argument with a challenge: "What are you waiting for?" He frames hesitation, not resources, as the remaining barrier.

- Tools, education, infrastructure and distribution channels are cheap or free.
- Waiting for funding, a better setup or permission is treated as avoidable delay.
- The implied action is to start building now with the cheapest available stack.

## Bootstrapping without funding

Pieter ties the argument to his book MAKE, which he describes as being about "building startups without funding." The low costs above are the practical basis for that approach:

- A bootstrapped founder needs no investor money to cover a laptop, a domain and a small server.
- Ongoing costs are low enough (a couple of dollars a month for hosting) that a project can run indefinitely while it finds traction.
- Keeping costs near zero removes the pressure to raise capital before validating an idea.
