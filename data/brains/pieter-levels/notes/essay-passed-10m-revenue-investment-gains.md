## Revenue stays flat while profit margin and investment returns do the compounding

- Pieter passed $10M/y in combined revenue and investment gains in 2026, at a profit margin of about 94.5%.
- His business revenue has been very stable at about $200K-$250K/mo.
- Investment returns have now surpassed business revenue, which he says has been true for about a year.
- His loop: "Spend less -> lower costs -> more profit -> more money saved -> more money to invest -> more % returns on investments".
- Most gains are unrealized because he never sells, and he flags that investments fluctuate, so the figure deserves "a big grain of salt".

## Cutting SaaS dependencies by vibecoding replacements

- Pieter has aggressively increased his profit margin for years, especially in the last few.
- This year he reduced his SaaS dependencies to just a handful of companies by vibecoding his own replacements.
- The result is the roughly 94.5% profit margin.
- He treats cost reduction as a first-class lever, equal to growing revenue.

## Investing profits: ETFs plus bets on suppliers he actually uses

- Most of his investments are simple ETFs, such as the Vanguard S&P500.
- Alongside those he made a few stock and startup bets that worked out.
- 2022: he couldn't get any available GPUs, reasoned there was a shortage, and bought Nvidia.
- 2023: Danny Postma recommended investing in the companies behind the AI inference for their photo AI apps. He invested first in Replicate and then in FAL.
- 2025: he invested in Cursor, which was then acquired by SpaceX recently.
- His tactic: "I just asked every app/service I used if I could invest".
- The pattern is to invest in the tools and vendors he depends on and sees demand for firsthand.

## Staying lean and profitable instead of spending to grow

- Many people over the last decade told him to spend all his money, personally or in the business, to grow it.
- He says he doesn't know how that would have ended up, and maybe his companies would be bigger.
- He is happy he took his "own lean profitable healthy way" and thinks it ended up great.

## Tracking profit margin and sleep next to revenue

- He keeps revenue and investment gains, profit margin, sleep score and last workout together in a "situation monitor" dashboard.
- He says these are "equally important", treating health metrics as on par with financial ones.
