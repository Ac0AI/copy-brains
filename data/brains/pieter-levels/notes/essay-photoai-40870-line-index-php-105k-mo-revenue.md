## Photoai.com's scale and revenue (March 2026)

- Pieter reports that photoai.com is a single file called `index.php` with 40,870 lines.
- It makes $105,000/mo in revenue and $80,000/mo in profit, per his post dated 6 March 2026.
- Implied margin from his figures: $80,000 profit on $105,000 revenue, so about $25,000/mo goes to costs.
- The post is deliberately minimal: the headline numbers are the whole message.

## Architecture: one PHP file as the whole product

- Pieter runs a profitable AI product from one monolithic PHP file rather than a framework, microservices or a multi-repo setup.
- The point he makes is that code structure and "clean architecture" are not what produce revenue. The product making $80,000/mo profit is a 40,870 line `index.php`.
- This matches his broader approach of building startups without funding, which he wrote up in his book MAKE.

## Takeaway for builders

- Choose the simplest stack that ships (plain PHP, a single file) and let revenue, not engineering aesthetics, decide when to refactor.
- A large, unglamorous codebase is not a blocker to strong profit: he cites $80,000/mo profit from it.
- Share concrete numbers publicly (revenue and profit) as proof. The post reached 1,560,497 views, 7,250 likes and 501 reposts on X.
