## The emoji timeline: VC startups vs bootstrapped startups

Pieter compares the two paths in emoji, a 2017 post that reached roughly 1,047,801 views, 4,497 likes and 1,747 reposts.

- VC startups: 💰 then ten 😁 (up to 😁😁😁😁😁😁😁😁😁), then 🔥💥 and finally 😫😫😫.
- Bootstrapped startups: 🤔🤓😮😧😵😞😒 then 💰 then 😏🙂😀😄😃😆😁.

## What the VC path looks like in his telling

- The money (💰) arrives first, at the very start.
- A long stretch of excitement and success feelings follows the funding.
- It ends in 🔥💥 (a blow-up or crash) and then three stressed faces (😫😫😫), meaning the pain comes last.
- Pieter's point: the high is front-loaded and the stress and burnout come at the end.

## What the bootstrapped path looks like in his telling

- It starts with thinking and tinkering (🤔🤓), then shock, worry, confusion and disappointment (😮😧😵😞😒).
- The money (💰) only arrives after that rough early stretch.
- After the money, the emotions climb steadily through smiling and laughing faces to a wide grin (😏🙂😀😄😃😆😁).
- Pieter's point: the pain is front-loaded and the payoff, in money and mood, comes at the end.

## The core takeaway

- The same ingredients (money and emotions) appear in both paths, but in reverse order.
- VC: cash first, then fun, then crash and stress.
- Bootstrapped: struggle first, then cash, then lasting happiness.
- This frames bootstrapping as the path Pieter prefers. He promotes his book MAKE as being about "building startups without funding".
