## Craigslist front page as a startup idea generator

Pieter's tactic for finding a startup idea is to go to the Craigslist front page and pick an item you can build a company around. The front page lists categories of real things people already buy, sell, hire for and look for, so it works as a ready-made list of proven markets.

- The method is simple. Browse the Craigslist front page and find an item or category that you could turn into a standalone company.
- The idea comes from existing demand, not from inventing a problem yourself.
- The implied step is to build a focused, better product around one slice of what Craigslist handles in a generic way (jobs, housing, services, for sale).

He shared this on 14 November 2018 as part of his advice on building startups without funding, the theme of his book MAKE.
