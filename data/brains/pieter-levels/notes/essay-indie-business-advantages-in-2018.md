## Why 2018 favors indie businesses over Big Tech

Pieter argues that people forget how much structural advantage an indie business has in 2018. He names three tailwinds working in a small builder's favor:

- People no longer trust Big Tech companies.
- People love to support small businesses.
- People love to follow a founder's journey and be part of it.

## Trust as the opening against Big Tech

- Pieter's first point is that distrust of Big Tech is now widespread ("People don't trust Big Tech companies anymore").
- The implication he draws is that an indie business starts with a positioning edge: it is the small, human, accountable alternative to a large company.
- He frames this as an advantage that is easy to overlook, not as something an indie has to build from scratch.

## Small business goodwill

- Pieter says people actively "love to support small businesses."
- Being small and independent is therefore an asset in how customers decide who to buy from, not a handicap to hide.
- Founders can lean into being small rather than trying to look like a large company.

## Building in public: the audience as participants

- Pieter says people "love to follow your journey and be part of it by helping you in any way (feedback, money)."
- The audience helps in two concrete ways he names: feedback and money.
- The journey itself works as a distribution and engagement asset. Sharing the story turns followers into contributors and customers.
- This is consistent with the approach of his book MAKE, about building startups without funding.
