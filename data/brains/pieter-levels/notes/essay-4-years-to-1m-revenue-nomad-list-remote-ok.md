## $83,333/mo revenue milestone: where Nomad List and Remote OK stood after 4 years

- In May 2019 Pieter announced that his two apps, Nomad List and Remote OK, had reached "$83,333+/mo revenue".
- He called $83k "special because * 12 is $1M", meaning $1M annual revenue.
- It took 4 years to get there.
- The snapshot: 11,996 customers, 740 payments per month, 208 cron jobs, $115 average payment, 2 apps, 1 VPS, $0 ad budget, $0 funding.
- The post got roughly 766k estimated views and 3,287 likes on X.

## The business model behind the number: few payments, high average ticket

- 740 payments per month at a $115 average payment is what produces roughly $83k/mo.
- The $115 average payment is high for consumer web products, so a modest count of payments is enough to reach $1M a year.
- The 11,996 customers figure shows the base built up over the 4 years.
- Pieter presents the two apps as the whole business: `nomadlist.com` and `remoteok.io`.

## Lean infrastructure: one VPS and 208 cron jobs

- The entire revenue base ran on 1 VPS.
- It used 208 cron jobs, which he lists as part of the stack alongside the 2 apps.
- Pieter treats this as a point of pride: a $1M/yr business with minimal servers and tooling.
- This fits his stated approach in the book *MAKE* of building startups without funding.

## Zero ad budget, zero funding

- He reached the milestone with "0 ad budget": no paid acquisition.
- He reached it with "0 funding": no investors, fully bootstrapped.
- Both are listed in the same stat block as the revenue figures, as part of the achievement rather than as footnotes.
- He frames the 4-year timeline as the cost of doing it this way.

## Takeaways Pieter highlights

- Slow, compounding progress: the $1M run rate took 4 years, across two products rather than one.
- Running multiple small apps on shared simple infrastructure can add up to seven figures a year.
- High per-payment value ($115 average) matters more than payment volume.
- No ads and no funding are presented as the baseline, not a handicap.
