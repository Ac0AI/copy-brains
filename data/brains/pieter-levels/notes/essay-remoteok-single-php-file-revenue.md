## RemoteOK's single-file architecture

- Pieter states that RemoteOK.io is "a single PHP file called 'index.php'" that generated $2,342.04 in one day (post dated 7 December 2017).
- He says it uses "No frameworks. No libraries."
- The point he is making: a profitable product does not require a framework, a dependency stack, or a complex architecture. A single file handled the whole site and earned over $2,300 in a day.

## Building simple to make money

- Pieter presents the single-file setup as a deliberate strength and celebrates it ("💖"), not as a limitation to apologize for.
- The post is a signal that revenue comes from the product and its distribution, not from technical sophistication.
- This is consistent with his book MAKE, which he describes as being about "building startups without funding."

## Specific revenue figure as proof

- He quotes revenue to the cent ($2,342.04) for a single day, a pattern of showing exact numbers in public to make the claim credible.
- The post drew roughly 381,916 estimated views, 1,639 likes and 645 reposts on X, showing how sharing real revenue and a contrarian tech choice drives attention to the product.
