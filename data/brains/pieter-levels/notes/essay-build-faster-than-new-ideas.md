## Pieter builds faster than he gets new ideas

- Pieter says it is "such a weird experience" that he can now build things faster than he gets new ideas for things to build.
- The bottleneck has flipped. Building used to be the slow, scarce step, and ideas were the cheap part. Now building is cheap and fast, and good ideas are the limiting factor.
