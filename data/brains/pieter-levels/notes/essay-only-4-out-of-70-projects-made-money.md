## Pieter's hit rate: roughly 5%, so the answer is to ship more

- Pieter reports that only 4 out of 70+ projects he has ever made earned money and grew.
- More than 95% of everything he made failed.
- He puts his hit rate at about 5%.
- His conclusion is short: "So...ship more."

## Treat failure as the normal outcome of building

- Pieter does not present failure as a rare event to avoid. He presents it as the base rate: roughly 19 out of 20 projects do not work.
- The post was written in November 2021, and he counted 70+ projects at that point.
- He frames the numbers as a plain statement of fact, not a story of regret.

## Volume is the strategy

- Since he cannot predict which project will be one of the few winners, he argues the way to find them is to make many projects.
- A low hit rate is acceptable when each attempt is cheap and fast to ship.
- The implication he draws is "ship more": more attempts mean more chances to land one of the roughly 5% that grow.

## Why this matters for a would-be founder

- Failed projects do not define the outcome. The few that made money did, according to his own track record.
- He measures success by projects that "made money and grew," not by how many he started.
- His position is that the only way to improve the odds of a hit is to increase the number of shipped attempts.
