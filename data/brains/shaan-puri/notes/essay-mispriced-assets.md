## Mispriced assets: look for what's overlooked or misunderstood

- Shaan's frame: "mispriced assets" are "things that are overlooked, or misunderstood." Stocks are one place to find them, but he looks for them in daily life too.
- Example: Weight Watchers stock. It was down 90%+, with a market cap of only $60M, for a company "that everyone and their mom has heard of."
- His read: weight loss didn't go away and wasn't a fad, since "we're still fatter than ever." Ozempic (and other factors) had crushed the stock.
- On the podcast he said it "looks mispriced" and that he'd buy some stock that day. A month later the stock was up +166%.
- He doesn't claim to be a stock-picking genius. The point is the habit of spotting assets the market has written off for the wrong reasons.

## Personality traits can be mispriced too

- Shaan asks which personality traits are undervalued.
- Being smart is fairly priced: "Everyone wants to be smart."
- His pick for an undervalued trait is showmanship. As people become "bent-neck nerds, staring at phones," old-school showmanship has become rare, which makes it scarcer and more valuable.

## Story: the free throw that made a coach's year

- Shaan, a 36-year-old out-of-shape tech guy, hired a basketball skills coach. His reason: running on a treadmill is miserable, and playing basketball makes him happy.
- The coach normally trains NBA prospects. Shaan showed up with greying hair, mismatched socks and a Michael Scott-style tank-top jersey.
- The coach asked for a free throw to see his form and baseline. Shaan dribbled, exhaled and threw up an intentionally ugly shot (a CLANK).
- After the coach's stunned reaction, Shaan paused, said "I'm just kidding man," shot a normal free throw and made it.
- The coach laughed and said it was his favorite moment at work all year. He told his coworkers and was still laughing about it at the next session.
- Shaan's takeaway: about 10 seconds of playfulness made someone's day and created a memorable impression.

## Don't let the world beat the playfulness out of you

- Adults tend to lose their playfulness and showmanship because "the world wants you to be serious."
- Shaan questions that: "Why so serious? Everybody likes fun." He thinks people treat fun as "wrong or inappropriate" when it isn't.
- His daily action: find a moment to play, and do something with "a little finesse. A little style. A little…showmanship!"
