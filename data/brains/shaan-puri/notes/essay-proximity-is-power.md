## Move to where the best soil is

- Shaan's one-word answer to a 27-year-old with a "good but not great" life in Nashville is "move". He says the easiest self-improvement lever is often changing your surroundings: "If you want flowers to bloom, plant your seeds in great soil."
- His own case: at 23 he was in Australia making a very cushy $120k/year with an easy job in a beautiful city. He quit and moved to San Francisco with no job and no contacts, because that is the best soil for startups.
- His conclusion: "proximity is power."

## Cities set the status game

- On his first day in SF he sat in a coffee shop next to a founder pitching a VC. The VC took 30-minute pitch meetings all day, and Shaan eavesdropped for 4 hours. He thought the ideas were terrible ("Uber for masseuses!").
- At a founder meetup that night, almost everyone had a "billion dollar idea". The exception was Craig, who worked at Deloitte and apologized and made excuses for it when introducing himself.
- His takeaway: SF is the only city where startups are normal and jobs are weird. "The high-status thing was low-status here. That's a powerful motivator."
- He cites Paul Graham: "every city whispers something in your ear."
  - LA whispers "you're not beautiful enough"
  - NYC whispers "you're not rich enough"
  - SF whispers "you're not ambitious enough"
- He says the whisper isn't always healthy, but it's always motivating. This is why actors move to Hollywood and startups move to SF.

## What moving does and how to pick where

- Moving shakes up your routine, social circle, pace, thinking and lifestyle, and forces you to re-think your routines.
- Where to move: "Go where your nerds are." Whatever you are nerdy about, find the place with the highest density of like-minded nerds.
- Example: to build muscle, live in a house with a bunch of gym bros. Soon you will have a gym routine, drink pre-workouts and guzzle protein.
- He frames a comfortable life as fine if that's what you want, but advises moving if you feel you are wasting your potential.
