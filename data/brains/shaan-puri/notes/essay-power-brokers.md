## The voice in your head is the real power broker

- Shaan opens with a question: who is the most important voice in your life? He rules out parents (huge influence for 18 years, then time with them drops suddenly), teachers (a few good ones, but not the most important), and friends (influential, since groups end up talking, dressing and aiming alike).
- His answer is "None of the above": "The most important voice in your life is the one that no one else hears." That voice is the director of your life's movie. It tells you what to say, where to stand, when to shut up, and what the story means.
- He cites studies that humans have up to ~60k thoughts per day, ~80% of which skew negative and ~95% of which are repetitive. These are "factory settings", and like a good toy you can change them to be what you want.

## Source idea: Jim Loehr's "power broker"

- Shaan credits the concept to Tim Ferriss, who told him he read *Mental Toughness Training For Sports* by Dr. Jim Loehr in a bunk bed at 17 and it changed his life.
- The book's core line, as Shaan quotes it: "The power broker in your life is the voice that no one ever hears. It is the master storyteller, and the stories we tell ourselves are our reality."
- Shaan's takeaway: the stories we tell ourselves are our reality.

## Shaan's podcast launch example

- When he launched his podcast he **hoped** it would be a hit. He pushed publish and waited. Nothing happened: no texts, no tweets, not even a bad review. "Nothing, nada, zilch."
- He then played the first minute himself, and his inner voice said: "Ew. Is that what I sound like? Why am I saying 'like' so many times? This sucks. I hope nobody hears this."
- His point: the voice can give you two opposite messages about any new thing you try, a discouraging one or an encouraging one. Same event, different story.

## Which story is true: whichever one you believe

- He argues the story you believe is the one that becomes true for you.
- Smarter people resist this because they think there is an objective "truth". He counters with Elon's line: "Physics is the law. Everything else is a suggestion."
- Everything in your life is neutral. "It's the meaning we put ON it that gives it a positive or negative spin."

## The practice: ask the voice for other stories

1. Notice the voice when it starts narrating.
2. Pause and ask it to produce alternative stories: what else is on the menu, what else could be true?
3. Pick the story that serves you.

- He closes with Garry Tan's line from MFM: "the key to life is realizing that it's all made up… but you get to make it up."
