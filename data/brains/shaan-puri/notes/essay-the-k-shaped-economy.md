## AI will not replace you, but it will split workers into two paths

- Shaan frames the future of work as "shaped like a K". Two paths diverge from the same starting point.
- Path one: you get replaced by AI. He compares this to being a horse after "Henry Ford just put a car on the road".
- Path two: you learn to use AI and become "10X more productive".
- His point is that the choice is yours. You either learn the tools or get displaced by someone who did.

## Treat your job as a bundle of tasks, not one big thing

- Shaan's core mental model: stop thinking of your job as one big thing. Think of it as a "bundle of tasks".
- Example, a sales rep's job breaks into:
  - Prospecting potential customers
  - Drafting cold emails
  - Tracking response rates
  - Scheduling calls
  - Doing the calls
  - Writing notes during the call
  - Follow-up emails
  - Drafting proposals
  - Updating the CRM
- The exercise is to list every task in your role, then ask which ones AI can do or boost today.
- Task-level thinking is what lets you see where AI helps instead of reacting to a vague fear of replacement.

## How much of a job AI can do now versus soon

- Shaan's rough estimate for the 20-task sales rep list: only "2-3 out of 20" tasks can be significantly boosted by AI today.
- He says AI is improving fast and that "soon, half your tasks will be handled by AI".
- Implication: the share of a job that is automatable grows over time, so the bundle you audit now should be re-audited regularly.
- Start with the few tasks AI can already boost, build the habit, and expand as capability grows.

## Managing robots will be worth more than managing humans

- Shaan's prediction, which he tells readers to "write this down": "in the future being able to 'manage robots' will be more valuable than managing humans."
- His advice is to learn AI tools and get good at directing them, to "make them your baby-back-b\*tch".
- The skill is delegating tasks from your bundle to AI the way a manager delegates to a team, then reviewing and steering the output.
- That ability is what puts you on the upper arm of the K.
