## Hiring a small "dream team" to explore before committing to an idea

Farza is assembling a 4-person team in San Francisco for a 3-month stint (rough dates: May 14 to Aug 14) to find a new company together. He is explicit that he doesn't yet know what the company is.

- Possible directions he floats: "maybe its an ai consumer app," "maybe it's a vr game," "maybe its a new lightbulb company."
- He says he is not at the stage where he can offer "some big beautiful vision" to get people hyped. For that, he points people to OpenAI or SpaceX.
- He describes the stage as "that early unknown stage no one really talks about," where you throw things out and see what resonates.
- He admits this phase "is not very sexy" and "can be exhausting."

## Why he's doing the early phase with a team instead of alone

- For his previous companies, he usually navigated the earliest, unclear stage alone, and only brought people on "once i feel like i have something that can float."
- This time he wants to do it differently, with others from day one.
- He believes in "the power of a small group of like-minded people essentially tinkering daily." In his view, this is "where every big vision or big company starts."
- The daily loop: get inspired, make something, share it, and try to find something completely new together.
- He considers this more exciting than starting from a fixed vision.

## The offer and terms

- 4 people, 3 months.
- Farza flies each person out and gets them a place in SF.
- $10k per month base pay.
- The team works together every day at the 42,000 sqft Founders Inc campus in Fort Mason, SF, which he calls home to "the best builders in the world."
- Applications are expected to number a few hundred; he says he can't reply to everyone, but those who look like a fit hear from him by May 1.
- Applications go through a Tally form.

## The four roles and why that mix

He wants one person for each of these:

- **Software person:** "an obsessed developer."
- **Film maker:** someone who wants to make movies.
- **Designer:** "you know how to make people feel."
- **Content creator:** "you know how to go viral."

His stated belief is that this group "can build nearly anything, and take over the world together." The mix covers building, making it feel good, telling the story, and distributing it.

## Success and failure conditions are set up front

- If in 3 months the team "makes magic" and reaches millions of users and hundreds of thousands in revenue, they keep going.
- If they get nothing, "the team will self-destruct. like team rocket from pokemon."
- Even in the failure case, he frames it as 3 months spent in "the most beautiful city in the world" trying their hardest.
- This is a time-boxed bet with a clear kill criterion, so nobody is stuck in a vague, open-ended project.

## Operating principles for the exploration phase

- "We'll be ruthless with ideas."
- "We will try a lot of things."
- "We will ship a lot."
- The goal is "to learn fast. and grow. not to look cool."
- Farza has been making things for 15 years and says this is a new company start, not a continuation of past ones.
